## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Car owners who care about their vehicle can't easily find a studio that does certified, long-lasting paint and glass protection — they're forced to choose between generic washes that don't protect anything or dealership packages that are overpriced and non-specialist.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic detailing/wash services: Convenient but no coating expertise, no lasting protection, risk of scratching
- Dealership packages: Overpriced, cookie-cutter, technicians aren't coating/PPF specialists
- DIY coating kits: Inconsistent results, incorrect prep, no warranty
- Cheap PPF installers: Poor film quality, visible seams, no self-healing property

**What it costs them (before finding Pristine):**
- Stone chips and swirl marks that devalue the car at trade-in
- Money spent on coatings or film that don't actually last
- Mental overhead of managing protection on a car they've invested $50K–$200K+ in

**Emotional tension:**
- "I've spent a lot on this car and I don't want to ruin the paint"
- "I don't know who to trust — everyone claims their coating is the best"
- "I keep meaning to get it protected but I never get around to it"

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "What's the difference between ceramic and graphene?" | Graphene bonds more densely than ceramic, giving better heat dissipation, scratch resistance, and an even slicker self-cleaning finish — it's the next step up. |
| "Isn't this expensive?" | A ceramic coating at $999 protects a $100K car for years and maintains resale value — it pays for itself. |
| "Can I combine PPF and ceramic/graphene?" | Yes — and we recommend it. PPF goes on first as a physical barrier, coating goes on top for the hydrophobic finish. |
| "Do you come to me?" | Coating and PPF work is done at our studio for quality control. Window tinting is the one service our mobile team installs at your home or office. |

**Anti-persona:** Owners of older/high-km vehicles not focused on resale value; price-shoppers who want the cheapest coating regardless of quality
