## Why November UV Is Different

Melbourne's UV index climbs sharply in November. The city sits at a latitude where spring and summer sun arrives at a steeper angle than in Sydney or Brisbane, and the proximity to Port Phillip Bay amplifies reflective UV intensity for bayside suburbs. Brighton, Sandringham, and Mordialloc regularly record UV indices above 11 (extreme) on clear November days — a level that breaks down unprotected automotive clear coat measurably over just a few months of repeated exposure.

Ceramic and graphene coatings form a UV-stable barrier over the clear coat. That barrier does the absorbing — not the paint itself.

## Bird Drops in November: The 10-Minute Clock

Bird droppings are acidic at pH 3–4. When Melbourne's surface temperatures reach 30°C or above — common from late November through February — that acid bakes into unprotected clear coat in under 10 minutes. The etching isn't visible immediately. It shows up as a dull, slightly pitted mark two to three weeks later, after the contamination has already penetrated the clear coat.

No amount of machine polishing restores paint that has been etched all the way through. If the damage is severe enough, the only fix is a panel respray.

A properly applied ceramic or graphene coating creates a sacrificial layer with significantly higher chemical resistance than clear coat alone. Bird drops still need to be rinsed off promptly — but the coating buys time and prevents penetration.

## November Is Still Within the Window — But Not For Long

Ceramic and graphene coatings cure best in controlled studio conditions with ambient temperatures between 10°C and 25°C. Melbourne's November temps sit squarely in that range — which makes this month the last reliable entry point before summer's 35°C+ days push coating application into more demanding territory.

This isn't a hard cutoff. Coatings can be applied in warmer conditions with the right preparation. But November bookings are filling 4–6 weeks out, which means December and January slots are either unavailable or limited.

If you plan to have your car protected before Christmas, the booking needs to happen now.
