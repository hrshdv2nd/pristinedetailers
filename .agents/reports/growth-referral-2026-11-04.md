## Melbourne Cup Day: Activate Now

Today is the single best organic referral trigger of the year. Cars are out in force. Toorak, South Yarra, Brighton — every car worth protecting is on the road or in a valet queue.

### Cup Day Opportunistic SMS

**Who:** All customers who've had a service in the last 12 months
**Channel:** SMS
**Send time:** Today, 11:00–11:30am (before racing starts; people are assembled, phones out)

---

```
Happy Melbourne Cup, [First Name].
Your car's looking its best today — and so is everyone else's.
If someone asks where you get it done, here's your link:
[referral link]
They book, you both get a complimentary Maintenance Detail. — Pristine
```

---

**Why today works:**
- It's not a generic promotion — it's grounded in a real Melbourne moment
- "Your car's looking its best today" is a compliment that lands because they just had it serviced
- The ask is natural: "if someone asks" is how referrals actually happen at social events
- Short. Fits in a notification preview. No CTA required beyond the link.

### Message 3 — Lapsed Customer Win-Back (Referral Frame)

**Who:** Customers who had a service 12–18 months ago and haven't rebooked.
**Channel:** Email
**Send:** Week of November 10

**Context:** A customer whose coating is 12–18 months old is approaching the annual maintenance window. The referral frame is: invite them to refer a friend before they rebook — if both come in together, they both benefit.

---

**Subject line (A/B test):**
- Option A: `Your coating's due for a check-in — and so is your mate's car`
- Option B: `It's been 12 months. Melbourne summer's starting. Here's an idea.`

**Preview text:** Come back together. You both save.

---

**Email body:**

---

Hi [First Name],

Your [ceramic/graphene] coating is about 12 months old. It's still doing its job — but November is a good time for a maintenance top-up before the heat and UV of a Melbourne summer hit.

Here's the other thing: if you know someone who's been meaning to get their car protected and hasn't moved on it yet, December is the last realistic window before Christmas bookings close us out.

**Refer them before you both book, and you each get a complimentary Maintenance Detail added to your service.**

[Referral link button: "Get Your Referral Link"]

One friend. One link. Two cars going into summer protected.

If you'd rather just rebook your own maintenance first, we're here:
[Booking link]

— The Pristine Team

*P.S. We work on [customer's car model] — if you've had any changes in the last year (new wheels, touch-up work, anything that's gone through the clear coat), mention it when you book and we'll factor it in.*

---

**Why this works:**
- The "12 months" framing is factual and timely — it doesn't feel like a generic win-back
- It gives two options: refer first, or rebook solo — the customer chooses
- The P.S. uses their car model, which signals care and reduces the "this is automated" feeling
- It reframes the win-back as a favour: "help a mate" not "come back and spend money"

## Referral Ask Scripts: Verbal Trigger at Pickup

The best referral ask in a premium service business is in-person. At car pickup — when the customer sees the finish for the first time — the technician has a 30-second window that no SMS can replicate.

**Script for technician at pickup:**

> "If anyone sees this and asks where you got it done, we'd love you to send them through. We'll give them a free Maintenance Detail on their first booking, and one for you too. Here — I'll text you the link while you're here."

**Why this works:**
- Timing: emotional peak (car looks incredible)
- Proximity: the car is the visual proof; no description needed
- Removes friction: technician sends the link immediately so the customer has it
- Framing: it's a gift to their friend, not a sales pitch

Add this as a standard step in the technician's end-of-service checklist.
