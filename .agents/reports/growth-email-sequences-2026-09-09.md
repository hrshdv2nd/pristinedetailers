## The Loop This Week Is Solving

A great Pristine Detailers job generates word-of-mouth **only if we ask at the right moment**. This email sequence stack is designed to catch four critical moments in the customer lifecycle:

1. **Booking confirmation** → reduce pre-job anxiety, set professional expectations, build trust before a single panel is touched
2. **Post-service follow-up** → capture the Google review while the finish is fresh, plant the referral seed
3. **30-day dormant win-back** → catch customers before their coating warranty is at risk, reactivate with genuine value
4. **Upgrade nudge (2+ bookings)** → loyal customers who've been in twice are primed for the full-stack recommendation

The loop: great job → review email → Google review published → new customer finds us → books → post-service email → refers a friend → that friend books → upgrade nudge closes the full-stack upsell → they become a brand ambassador.

## Email 1: Post-Booking Confirmation

**Trigger:** Immediately after booking is confirmed
**Sequence type:** Post-purchase / onboarding
**Goal:** Build trust, set precise expectations, reduce pre-job anxiety

---

**Subject:** Your [Service] is confirmed — here's what happens on the day
**Preview:** Studio prep starts before you arrive. The full picture below.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

You're booked in. Here's exactly what to expect.

**Before you arrive**
Our technician sets up the studio ahead of your slot — decontamination bay, coating environment, and any film cutting is prepared specifically for your car. You won't be waiting around while we get organised.

**On the day**

We start with a full decontamination wash. This removes bonded surface contaminants that most studios either skip or don't know how to address — it's also why those coatings don't last.

From there, depending on your service:

- **Ceramic or Graphene coating:** Panel inspection → machine polish (if included) → coating application in a controlled environment → initial cure
- **PPF:** Panel-by-panel film application → edge wrapping → squeegee and heat cure
- **Window tinting:** Film cut and application → squeegee out → cure time

**Collection**
We'll send you a collection SMS when your car is ready. For ceramic and graphene coatings: keep the car dry for the first 12–24 hours. No rain, no car wash, no wet garage. We'll confirm any service-specific care notes on the day.

If anything comes up before your appointment, reply to this email. You'll reach a technician, not a booking system.

See you [Appointment Date].

[Technician Name]
Pristine Detailers

## Email 3: 30-Day Dormant Win-Back

**Trigger:** 30 days since last completed booking, no new booking in CRM
**Sequence type:** Re-engagement / retention
**Goal:** Reactivate customer with genuine maintenance value; position annual maintenance coating and add-ons

---

**Subject:** Your coating at the 30-day mark — one thing worth checking
**Preview:** Before Melbourne summer hits, there's one maintenance step most people skip.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

It's been about a month since your [Service] at Pristine. Wanted to check in with something useful.

**Your coating is doing exactly what it should.** Stone chips, UV, bird droppings — they're hitting the coating surface, not your paint. But a few things can shorten its working life if left unchecked:

**1. Washing technique**
Your ceramic or graphene coating doesn't make the car immune to swirl marks. Automated brushes and dirty wash mitts still scratch the coating. Hand wash with a clean microfibre mitt, two-bucket method, and you're fine.

**2. Iron contamination on wheels**
Brake dust deposits can bond to your coating and wheels over time. An iron decontamination spray every 3–6 months keeps them clean. We stock it if you need a recommendation.

**3. Annual maintenance coating**
Most manufacturer warranties (Ceramic Pro, Gtechniq) require a maintenance coating applied annually to keep the warranty valid. It's a 2–3 hour studio visit. If you're coming up to 12 months, this is worth booking in before summer.

If your car is ready for a maintenance session — or you've been thinking about adding a **Glass Coating, Leather Ceramic, or Wheel Coating** — now is the right time before Melbourne summer arrives.

[→ Book a maintenance visit or add-on]

If everything is still turning heads, that's what we're here for.

Pristine Detailers

**If you're on ceramic coating and want to go further:**

Graphene is the next step up — and it's a meaningful one. The molecular bonding is denser than ceramic, which gives you better heat dissipation (relevant in Melbourne summers), stronger resistance to light scratches, and a more hydrophobic surface that stays cleaner for longer between washes.

The difference is noticeable. It's not marketing language.

Graphene coating from **$999**. If you've had ceramic done here, your paint prep history is already on file — we know your car's surface and can plan the coating accordingly.

**If you haven't added PPF yet:**

PPF is the physical barrier that handles all of that.

The full-stack approach — **PPF on vulnerable panels, then graphene coating over the top** — means your paint is protected from every direction. It's what we'd recommend for any car worth protecting.

PPF from **$3,000**. We do the full combination regularly; the installs are sequenced so the film and coating bond correctly.

## Implementation Notes

- `[First Name]` — from booking record
