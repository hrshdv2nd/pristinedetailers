## Victoria's Legal Tinting Limits

Every car on Victorian roads must comply with the Australian Design Rules (ADR) and VicRoads requirements for light transmission. VLT — Visible Light Transmission — is the percentage of light that passes through the glass. Lower VLT means darker tint.

| Window | Minimum VLT | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Windscreen | Factory glass only — no aftermarket film except a strip on the top 10% | Any applied film below that strip is non-compliant |
| Front side windows (driver and passenger) | 35% VLT minimum | Measured with a photometer at roadworthy inspection |
| Rear side windows | No minimum | Any VLT permitted |
| Rear windscreen | No minimum | Reversing camera required if rear vision is impaired |

The front windows are where most people get caught. A 20% VLT looks right on a show car; it will fail a roadworthy. We fit only compliant film by default. If you want front glass below 35%, we'll install it and document that it's your specific instruction.

## How Mobile Window Tinting Works

The process is straightforward. Here's what happens from booking to drive-away:

1. **Book online** — select your vehicle type, coverage (front only, rear only, full car), and a date that works.
2. **We confirm the location** — your driveway, office car park, or apartment bay. We need around 2 metres of clearance on each side and no direct sunlight on the glass during the job.
3. **Technician arrives with all equipment** — cut film rolls, squeegees, heat guns, cleaning kit. No water outlet required on your end.
4. **Glass preparation** — existing adhesive residue, silicone, and dirt are removed from the interior surface. This step determines whether the film bonds cleanly or lifts at the edges over time.
5. **Film application** — each pane is cut to size, a slip solution applied, and the film squeegeed flat. Edges are heat-shrunk to conform to curved glass without bubbles or lifting corners.
6. **Inspection and cure** — every pane is checked under direct light before we pack up. Full cure takes 3–5 days depending on ambient temperature. Don't lower the windows during this period.

Total time: 1.5 to 3.5 hours depending on vehicle size and coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does window tinting take?**
Most passenger cars take 1.5 to 2.5 hours for a full tint. SUVs and vehicles with large rear glass run 2.5 to 3.5 hours.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes — provided there's reasonable clearance on both sides and the space isn't actively dusty. We work in apartment car parks across South Yarra, Richmond, and Toorak regularly.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Window tinting is a modification to the glass surface only — it has no effect on drivetrain, electronics, or structure. It does not touch any component covered by manufacturer warranty. Some luxury brands offer dealer-fit tinting packages; our certified film is equivalent quality or better.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
35% VLT minimum for front side windows under VicRoads regulations. We fit compliant film on every build by default. If you specifically request a darker front window, we'll install it and note it's a non-compliant modification at your instruction.
