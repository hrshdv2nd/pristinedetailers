## The Winter Activation Angle

July in Melbourne is paint's worst month. Rain on the Mornington Peninsula, road salt from gravel paths in Bayside, bird droppings concentrated under winter roosting spots in Hawthorn and Toorak, UV-free days that make owners think protection can wait.

This is exactly wrong — and customers who know us know it. That gap is a referral window.

### Why winter referrals convert better than spring referrals

- Friends with coated cars visibly notice the difference in winter: water-beading vs. watermarks, bead-up vs. smear
- The "I've been meaning to sort out my car's paint" feeling is strongest after a wet week
- Spring is when demand spikes and booking slots tighten — locking a new member in now secures them ahead of the rush
- A member who starts in winter will be hitting their 6-month milestone right at peak season (January) — maximum satisfaction, maximum ambassador potential

**The referral trigger for winter:** Not the car looking its best — it's the contrast with *everyone else's car looking its worst.*

## Referral Ask Message 2: Existing Member Reactivation — July Push

**Context:** The June 3 member email asked for referrals. Some didn't act. This is the July touchpoint — different angle, higher urgency.  
**Channel:** Email

Hi [First Name],

Bit of an unusual email from us.

We don't often have a "best time to refer" — but July is it.

Here's why: spring is our busiest period. September and October are when half of Melbourne suddenly decides it's time to sort the car. Slots go fast. Members with priority booking get first access. Everyone else waits.

If you have a mate with a car worth protecting — a colleague, a neighbour, someone from your car club — and you've been meaning to mention us, now is genuinely the right time. If they join a membership before 1 September, they lock in:

- Priority spring slot access (before the rush)
- A complimentary Maintenance Detail to start
- You get a free Maintenance Detail too

No forms. No voucher codes. Reply to this email with their name and number and we'll reach out, mention you sent them, and take it from there.

If they're not a fit, don't send them. But if they have a car they care about and they keep putting off the maintenance — this is the call to action they probably need.

— The Pristine team

*PS — if you've already referred someone in the last few months, thank you. If they haven't joined yet, reply and we'll check in on where they're at.*

---

**Why this works differently from the June email:**
- Opens with a seasonal rationale ("July is the right time") — not just "hey, referral program"
- The postscript is operationally clever: some referred friends may have been contacted but not converted — this re-engages without re-asking
- "If they're not a fit, don't send them" — anti-sell language that paradoxically increases trust and share rate
- No repeat of "doing your friend a favour" framing (overused in June) — replaces it with "giving them a spring advantage"

## New: The In-Service Verbal Ask — Technician Script

The strongest referral moment happens in person, not via SMS 24 hours later. Technicians finishing a job in a driveway in Brighton or a car park in South Yarra are standing next to the product and the proud customer simultaneously.

Most don't ask. They should.

**Technician script (to say while packing up):**

> "Really happy with how that came up — the [coating / paintwork / wheels] on this one is exactly what we want to see. If you've got a mate with a car like this, we've got a program where you both get a free maintenance wash when they join a membership. Just let us know — it's one text to the office."

**What this does:**
- Delivered at peak satisfaction (car just finished, technician present, result visible)
- Framed as "a car like this" — speaks to the customer's pride in their vehicle
- The ask is minimal: "just let us know" — no commitment required on the spot
- Technician delivers it authentically, not as a script — keep the language natural

**Train this at next team catch-up. One sentence. Three minutes.**
