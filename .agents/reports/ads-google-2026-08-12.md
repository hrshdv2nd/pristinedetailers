**Platform:** Google Ads — Responsive Search Ads (RSA)

## Service Context

Ceramic coating creates a nano-ceramic chemical bond with the clear coat — hydrophobic, UV-stable, scratch-resistant. Pristine's mobile service means no drop-off: certified technicians come to the customer's home, office, or car park across Toorak, South Yarra, Bayside, Brighton, Hawthorn, and surrounds.

**Primary fear addressed:** Stone chips on freeways, bird droppings, UV fading, car park scratches — Melbourne's environment is aggressive on unprotected paint.

## Campaign Structure Recommendation

```
Campaign: GOOG_Search_Ceramic-Coating_Melbourne
├── Ad Group 1: [Ceramic Coating Melbourne] — exact match anchors
│   └── RSA: Headlines 1–5 above, both descriptions
├── Ad Group 2: [Mobile Ceramic Coating] — differentiator keywords
│   └── RSA: Headline 2 pinned to position 1, new support headlines
└── Ad Group 3: [Geo-Specific Postcodes] — Toorak, Bayside, Brighton
    └── RSA: Location-callout variants (write separately if budget allows)
```

**Location targeting:** Victoria — Inner East and Bayside suburb radius. Set 10km radius around 3142 (Toorak) and 3186 (Brighton) as primary zones.

**Ad schedule:** Run 6am–9pm Mon–Sat. Suppress Sunday mornings (low conversion data from other Melbourne mobile service businesses).

**Budget note:** Minimum $30/day to exit learning phase within 2 weeks. If CPA target is $80–120/lead (ceramic quote inquiry), that's realistic at $30–50/day once conversion data is in.
