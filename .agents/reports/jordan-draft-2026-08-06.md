## What the Law Requires in Victoria

Victoria's Road Safety (Vehicles) Regulations 2009 set hard limits on window tint darkness. Non-compliance is a roadworthiness issue — fail a roadworthy check or attract a police stop, and you're looking at a defect notice.

| Window Position | Minimum VLT | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Windscreen | Factory tint strip only | Cannot be aftermarket tinted |
| Front side windows (driver + passenger) | ≥35% VLT | 35% or lighter only |
| Rear side windows | No minimum | Any darkness level is legal |
| Rear windscreen | No minimum | Any darkness level is legal |

A 35% VLT film on the front means 35% of visible light passes through — noticeably darker than factory glass, but still legal and comfortable to drive at night. Most customers choose 20% or 5% on the rear for near-complete privacy. Our technicians advise on which level suits your vehicle's existing glass and your use case before a single sheet of film is cut.

## How Mobile Window Tinting Works

The mobile process takes between 1.5 and 3.5 hours depending on vehicle size and glass count. You don't need a workshop bay — a covered driveway, an apartment car park with decent lighting, or an office car park all work. Here's what happens on the day:

1. **Glass prep** — the technician cleans all panels with an ammonia-free solution to remove contamination, oils, and residue
2. **Film cutting** — film is sized for each window using a digital template or manual cut, whichever produces cleaner edges on your vehicle
3. **Application** — film is installed wet, squeegeed flat, and trimmed to precise edges
4. **Cure review** — the technician checks for bubbles and explains the cure timeline (typically 3–5 days before the film fully adheres)
5. **Final inspection** — every window checked from inside and outside before the technician packs up

You can stay nearby or get on with your day. Most customers are driving the car again the same afternoon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does window tinting take?**
Most vehicles take 1.5 to 3.5 hours. A standard four-door sedan runs about 2 hours. An SUV or wagon with more glass panels runs closer to 3 hours. We confirm exact timing at booking once we know the vehicle.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. Apartment car parks and basement bays work provided the lighting is reasonable and there's enough room to move around the vehicle. We've completed installations in South Yarra, Richmond, and Hawthorn car parks without issue. If your building requires contractor pre-authorisation, notify building management before booking.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Under Australian Consumer Law, a dealer cannot void your vehicle warranty because you've had aftermarket film applied. If your dealer suggests otherwise, that's an ACL breach. The only scenario where warranty could be affected is physical damage caused by the installation — which doesn't occur with professional fitting.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
Front side windows — the driver and passenger doors — must have a minimum of 35% VLT. The windscreen must not be aftermarket tinted, except for the factory-approved tint strip at the top edge. Rear glass has no legal minimum in Victoria.
