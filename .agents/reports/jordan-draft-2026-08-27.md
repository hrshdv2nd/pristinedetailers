## What Is Mobile Window Tinting?

Mobile window tinting means a technician brings all the equipment to your location — home, office, or apartment car park — and applies the film on-site. No workshop drop-off. No Moorabbin industrial estate. No organising a lift home.

The film reduces UV transmission, cuts cabin heat, and improves privacy. It also reduces glare, which matters for Melbourne drivers on the Eastern Freeway or Monash during peak afternoon sun. It's one of the more underrated services for daily drivers, and one of the most-requested additions since we launched it in July 2026.

## Victorian Legal VLT Limits

VLT stands for Visible Light Transmission — the percentage of light that passes through the glass. Higher VLT means more transparent; lower VLT means darker.

In Victoria the rules are:

| Window position | Minimum VLT |
|---|---|
| Windscreen (front) | ≥35% VLT (factory glass + film combined) |
| Front side windows | ≥35% VLT (combined) |
| Rear side windows | No minimum — any darkness permitted |
| Rear window | No minimum — any darkness permitted |

This catches a lot of drivers out. The 35% limit applies to the *total* VLT through the combined glass stack — not just the film alone. If your factory glass sits at 70% VLT and you apply a 50% film, the combined result is roughly 35%. You're at the legal limit.

Going darker than that on front windows will fail a VicRoads inspection and can result in a defect notice. Rear windows are different — there's no lower limit.

Our technicians check factory glass VLT with a calibrated meter before recommending a film grade. We don't guess.

## How the Mobile Process Works

1. **Assessment** — we check existing VLT on your windows using a calibrated light meter. Factory glass varies significantly by make and model.
2. **Surface preparation** — every window is cleaned and decontaminated. Dust or grease under the film causes bubbles.
3. **Film cutting** — precision-cut to each window's pattern using a plotter. No freehand cuts on finished glass.
4. **Application** — film is wet-applied, squeegeed flat, and edge-sealed.
5. **Cure time** — 3–5 days for the adhesive to fully bond. Don't wind the windows down during this period.

Total appointment time: 1.5–3 hours depending on vehicle size and coverage.

## FAQ

**How long does window tinting take?**
1.5–3 hours on-site, depending on vehicle type and coverage. Most jobs are completed in a single visit.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. We work in underground car parks across Melbourne — South Yarra, Richmond, Hawthorn, Toorak. We need adequate lighting and door clearance. Let us know the ceiling height when you book and we'll confirm access.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Window film is a surface application and doesn't affect any mechanical warranty. We haven't encountered a conflict in six years of operation.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
35% VLT minimum through the combined glass and film stack. We check factory glass first and recommend a compliant film grade before we touch anything.

**When can I wind my windows down after tinting?**
After 3–5 days. The adhesive needs time to cure fully. Winding them down early can lift the film edges at the seals.
