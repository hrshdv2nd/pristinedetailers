**Caption:**

Most people asking about paint protection end up choosing between ceramic and graphene coating — and most aren't sure what actually differentiates them beyond the price point. Here's the technical explanation that matters for asset protection.

Ceramic coatings form a nano-silica bond with the vehicle's clear coat. The result is a chemically hardened, hydrophobic, UV-stable layer that repels water, contaminants, and bird acid etch for up to 8 years under a manufacturer warranty. It is the proven standard across the industry, and for most vehicles, it is the right choice.

Graphene coatings use graphene-infused chemistry to achieve a denser molecular bond. This translates to three measurable improvements: superior heat dissipation (relevant for dark-coloured vehicles in direct sun — a real issue for cars parked outdoors in Melbourne's CBD or Bayside through summer), higher scratch resistance against the micro-abrasion caused by car parks and dust, and a self-cleaning finish that performs better in Melbourne's variable weather. Warranty extends to 9 years. For a vehicle worth $100K–$200K+, the ROI case for graphene over ceramic is straightforward: lower long-term maintenance cost, better resale presentation, and a finish that holds its condition longer.
