# Mobile Car Detailing in Melbourne: How It Works, What You Need, and How to Book

You need a 240V power point and an outdoor tap within 15 metres. We operate across 60+ Melbourne suburbs — your home, office, or apartment car park. Book online, we show up with everything else. No workshop. No drop-off. No waiting room.

![A Pristine Detailers technician performing a professional car detail](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1617469767824-e76ad64e9f86?w=1200&q=80)

## How Mobile Car Detailing Works

Mobile detailing means a certified technician brings professional-grade equipment to your location — pressure washer, dual-action polisher, decontamination chemicals, and everything needed for a full studio-quality result.

The process depends on the service you book:

- **Maintenance Detail ($99+):** Pre-rinse, two-bucket hand wash, decontamination spray, seal. 1.5–2 hours.
- **Basic Detailing ($150+):** Everything above plus interior vacuum, glass clean, and tyre dressing. 2–3 hours.
- **Revitalise Package ($300+):** Full decontamination, two-stage machine polish, deep clean, 6-month sealant. 4–5 hours.
- **Ceramic Coating ($750+):** Decontamination, paint correction prep, nano-ceramic application. 5–8 hours depending on vehicle size.
- **PPF Installation ($3,000+):** Partial or full film application, quoted per vehicle. 4 hours to 2 days.

The technician parks near your driveway or car park, connects to water and power, and works through the vehicle systematically. You don't need to be present for most of the service — more on that below.

## What You Need to Provide

The short answer: not much.

| Access requirement | What's needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Standard 240V power point | Outdoor or garage socket works; extension lead is our problem |
| Water | Outdoor tap within 15 metres | We carry our own hose connections |
| Space | Enough room to work around the vehicle | One full metre clearance on each side |
| Surface | Concrete, paving, or firm gravel | Wet grass or sand doesn't work for PPF or machine polishing |

If you're not sure your setup qualifies, describe your car park or driveway when you enquire — we'll tell you straight.

## Melbourne Apartments: Underground Car Parks and Strata Buildings

This is the question we get most from residents in South Yarra, Richmond, Prahran, Hawthorn, and Toorak. Yes, we service vehicles in underground car parks and strata-managed buildings — but it needs a few additional steps.

For underground car parks, we need:

- A strata-approved access time (usually arranged by the resident beforehand)
- Access to a 240V power point in or near the car park (most modern Melbourne apartment buildings have them)
- A water source — usually a hose bib or cold-water tap on the ground level, which we can run a hose from

![A prestige car parked on a Melbourne city street](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1568605117036-5c5edba50dc0?w=1200&q=80)

Not every service suits underground locations. A full paint correction or PPF installation requires controlled lighting and a clean environment — better suited to a driveway or space with good natural light. Maintenance details and basic washes work fine underground.

If you're in a South Yarra apartment block, a building off Chapel Street in Prahran, or anywhere in Richmond or Hawthorn, contact us before booking. We've navigated Melbourne's strata rules before and can tell you quickly whether your building works.

## How to Book

## FAQ

**Can you come to my underground car park?**
Yes, in most cases. We need a power point and water access within reach. Let us know your building address when you book and we'll confirm access before the appointment. Most Inner East apartment buildings qualify.

**What if I don't have outdoor water access?**
Contact us before booking. In some cases we can work with a kitchen or laundry tap via a window — it depends on the service and your building layout. PPF and ceramic coating typically require a proper outdoor water source.

**Do I need to be home during the detail?**
No. Most customers leave keys and head to work. We'll contact you when we're done, or if anything unexpected comes up mid-service. For first-time bookings we ask you to be reachable by phone during the appointment.

**Can you park in our strata car park?**
Technicians drive a van, so we need either a strata-approved visitor bay or street access nearby. Most Inner East and Bayside buildings have at least one option. Give us your address when you enquire and we'll sort out the logistics.
