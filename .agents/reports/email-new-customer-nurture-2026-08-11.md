## Email 2 — Day 7: Winter care tips for your coating

```
Send: Day 7 post-service
Subject: One week in — a few things to know this winter
Preview: Melbourne winter does specific things to paint. Here's how to stay ahead of it.
```

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Your coating should be fully cured by now — which means it's doing its job around the clock, even in this weather.

Winter in Melbourne is quieter on UV, but it brings its own set of risks. A few things worth knowing:

**Condensation on cold mornings.** When temperatures drop overnight and you run a hot engine, moisture can lift bird dropping acids faster than it would in summer. If you see droppings on the paint in the morning, get them off within a few hours — not a few days. Your coating makes this easy: a quick spray of diluted car shampoo and a soft cloth is all you need.

**If you park near the bay.** Salt air from Port Phillip doesn't corrode a ceramic-coated car like it would bare paint, but it does accumulate on the surface over time. A monthly rinse is enough to flush it off.

**What to avoid:** Automatic car washes with brushes. Even with a coating, rotating brushes introduce micro-scratches that dull the finish over time. A contactless wash is fine. A hand rinse at home is better.

Your coating is designed to make maintenance easier — not give you more things to worry about. The short version: rinse monthly, deal with bird droppings quickly, skip the brush car wash.

In a couple of weeks I'll reach out about your first maintenance check-in. In the meantime, if the car looks off to you or you have questions, just hit reply.
