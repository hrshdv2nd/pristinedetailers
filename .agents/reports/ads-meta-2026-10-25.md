## Targeting Recommendation

### Primary Audiences

**Audience A — Prestige Car Owners, Inner East + Bayside**
- Postcodes: 3141 (South Yarra), 3142 (Toorak), 3122 (Hawthorn), 3121 (Richmond), 3144 (Malvern), 3186 (Brighton), 3191 (Sandringham), 3182 (St Kilda)
- Age: 28–55
- Interests: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Tesla, Audi, car detailing, car care and maintenance, automotive accessories, luxury cars
- Behaviour: Auto intenders (high-value vehicle), frequent travellers (proxy for disposable income)

**Audience B — Mornington Peninsula Car Enthusiasts**
- Postcodes: 3931 (Mornington), 3936 (Rye), 3939 (Portsea/Sorrento corridor)
- Age: 35–60
- Interests: Porsche Club of Australia, weekend driving, prestige vehicles, car shows, coastal lifestyle
- Note: High density of track-day and collector cars in this corridor; weekend car movement = stone chip anxiety

### Lookalike Approach

1. **1% LAL** of booking page converters (best signal — actual intent)
2. **2% LAL** of email subscriber list (ceramic/graphene/PPF past customers)
3. Exclude: existing members, anyone who converted in last 90 days

### Placement
- Feed (Facebook + Instagram) for V1 and V3
- Reels / Stories for V2 (punchy sentence structure plays well in vertical)
- Audience Network: off (too low intent for this price point)

## Creative Direction Notes

- **Visual:** Before/after of a coated panel — water beading on a dark (black or navy) European car roof. Real, not stock. Toorak streetscape or bayside driveway background.
- **Avoid:** Generic "shiny car" stock imagery. Pristine's audience sees through it.
- **V2 visual:** Close-up of a bird dropping on paint — visceral, grabs the ICP immediately.
- **Format priority:** Static image first (test fastest), Reels format for V2 with street voiceover.
