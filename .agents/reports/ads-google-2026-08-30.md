## Seasonal Context

1 September is the first day of meteorological spring in Melbourne. This is the single best day of the year to launch a paint correction campaign.

**The "winter damage audit" buyer.** By the end of August, a Melbourne car owner who cares about their paint has five months of accumulated damage to reckon with: road spray, water spots from wet car parks in South Yarra or the CBD, contamination from Mornington Peninsula gravel, and the slow oxidation that sets in when sealant layers are not refreshed. The paint correction buyer isn't looking for a wash — they can see the swirl marks. They ran their hand across the bonnet and felt the roughness. They looked at their Porsche or Range Rover in direct spring sunlight and saw what winter did. That's the moment of intent. This campaign captures it.

**The "pre-coating" buyer.** Paint correction is the required step before a ceramic coating is applied. A car owner researching ceramic coating in Melbourne — whether they found Pristine through organic search or word of mouth — will inevitably learn that correcting the paint first locks in the result. The search journey for this buyer often moves from "ceramic coating Melbourne" to "paint correction before ceramic coating" before landing on "paint correction Melbourne." This campaign should own all three legs of that path. The description that frames paint correction as the prerequisite for a lasting ceramic coat speaks directly to this buyer.

**The "spring reset" buyer.** Melbourne's social calendar accelerates sharply in September: AFL Grand Final, the start of spring racing, weekend drives to Sorrento, the Porsche Club runs returning to the Peninsula. A Toorak or Brighton owner who wants their car looking right for the Birdcage or a Saturday on the Great Ocean Road starts taking stock of the car's condition in the first week of spring. For this buyer, the search is not "paint correction" in the abstract — it is the recognition that swirl marks and oxidation are visible in the October light in a way they were not visible in June. Paint correction is the reset before the season begins.

These three audiences share a search window in the first two weeks of September. The ad groups below address them.

"Book now" is a direct CTA — not "learn more," not "get a quote," because the buyer who has reached this ad and read the description is ready to transact, not to research further.

## Keyword Recommendations

### Exact Match — 5 Keywords

```
[paint correction Melbourne]
[swirl mark removal Melbourne]
[car scratch removal Melbourne]
[machine polish Melbourne]
[paint correction before ceramic coating]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[paint correction Melbourne]` — primary intent term; the buyer has named the service and the city; this is the anchor keyword for the entire campaign and must be owned with the tightest match type; bidding broad on this term will bleed spend into irrelevant modifiers (paint protection, paint correction kits, paint correction jobs listings)
- `[swirl mark removal Melbourne]` — the buyer's description of the problem, not the solution; car owners in Toorak or Brighton who ran their hand across the bonnet and noticed texture are more likely to search by the symptom ("swirl marks") than the service name ("paint correction"); this term captures the intent at the moment of problem identification, before the buyer has learned the service terminology
- `[car scratch removal Melbourne]` — broader damage framing; a buyer who sees a light scratch from a car park door ding in Prahran or South Yarra may not know whether they need paint correction or panel work; this term captures them at the top of the decision funnel; ad copy should qualify (machine polish for light scratches and swirl marks, not deep chips or structural damage) to avoid drawing panel-beater intent
- `[machine polish Melbourne]` — technical term used by buyers who have done research; a buyer who types "machine polish" has already ruled out hand wax and generic mobile washes; they know what they're looking for and are comparing providers on skill and credibility; "Certified Polish" in H4 speaks directly to this searcher
- `[paint correction before ceramic coating]` — long-tail, high-intent term from the pre-coating buyer mid-research journey; lower search volume but highest conversion likelihood; the buyer typing this phrase is one step away from booking both services; landing page should include a ceramic coating upsell pathway visible above the fold for this traffic segment

---

### Negative Keywords — 3 to Exclude

```
[DIY paint correction]
[paint correction course]
[panel beater Melbourne]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[DIY paint correction]` — explicit self-service intent; buyer is looking to do this themselves with a random orbital and a YouTube tutorial; they will not pay a technician; click-through from this term wastes budget and signals poor relevance to Google's auction, degrading Quality Score for the target keywords
- `[paint correction course]` — buyer wants to learn the skill, not hire someone; common search among enthusiasts and aspiring detailers; they are the anti-persona for a premium mobile service that the ICP values precisely because they don't want to do it themselves
- `[panel beater Melbourne]` — structural paint and bodywork intent (dents, collision repairs, rust); incompatible with cosmetic paint correction; a buyer whose paint problem requires a panel beater is not the buyer for Pristine's machine polishing service, and showing the ad against this term erodes Quality Score while attracting unqualified traffic that will bounce immediately on seeing the service description

## Context Notes

- **Seasonal timing:** 1 September is the first day of spring. Melbourne's spring sunlight makes paint defects visible that were concealed under grey winter skies. The purchase window for paint correction is more concentrated than any other service — it peaks in the first two weeks of September when car owners are reassessing their vehicle after winter. This campaign should have its highest daily budgets allocated to 1–14 September.
- **Melbourne-specific hooks used:** Toorak, Brighton, South Yarra, Prahran, Sorrento/Peninsula in geographic and copy context; spring racing / AFL Grand Final implicit in seasonal timing but not referenced in ad copy (too niche to be the primary hook); car park scratches in the CBD and South Yarra are real, specific objections for this service
- **Tone check:** "Machine polish removes swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation" — specific, not promotional. "Required before ceramic coating" — direct and informative, not salesy. "Certified Polish" — technical credibility, not generic "best." "We come to you" — factual differentiator, not a feature claim. No "premium," no "passionate," no "best in Melbourne."
- **Paint correction vs other services:** This ad set does not promote ceramic coating (next cycle) or PPF. Paint correction buyers are at a different decision stage — they've identified a visible problem and want it fixed. Mixing in coating or film messages dilutes intent matching and confuses the buyer about the service they're booking. The upsell pathway (described above) captures the coating opportunity post-conversion, not in the ad itself.
- **Pricing transparency:** "From $300" in H4 is deliberate — it anchors price for the buyer who has no reference point. The Revitalise Package at $300+ includes decontamination, two-stage paint correction, and a 6-month sealant. Naming the price in the headline qualifies the buyer before the click: anyone who clicks "From $300" already accepts that price range, reducing bounce rate and improving conversion quality.
