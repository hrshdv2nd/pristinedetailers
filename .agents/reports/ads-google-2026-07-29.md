## Seasonal Context

Late July is the right time to push membership for two reasons:

1. **Winter maintenance backlog.** Melbourne's July has delivered cold mornings, wet roads, and bird-dropping season in full force across Toorak, Hawthorn, and Bayside. Owners who skipped maintenance from April onward are now staring at a car that's overdue. The mental friction of rebooking keeps them stuck — membership removes that friction permanently.

2. **EOFY new car owners at the six-week mark.** June EOFY purchases are now six weeks old. The "I'll sort detailing eventually" owner is losing that new-car feeling. A membership offer frames ongoing care as automatic — they join once, and the problem is solved indefinitely.

Both audiences respond to the same core message: **you don't have to think about this again.**

**Pin guidance:**
- Pin H1 ("Membership Detailing Melbourne") to Position 1 — the buyer searching "car detailing membership Melbourne" or "mobile detailing plan Melbourne" is already looking for this product; the headline must confirm Pristine offers exactly what they want, not make them guess
- H2 and H3 rotate in Position 2 — H2 ($99/mo) answers the immediate "what does it cost?" question before the buyer reaches the landing page; H3 (35% savings) converts deliberate comparers who are calculating whether one-off bookings or a plan makes more financial sense
- H4 and H5 rotate in Position 3 — H4 names the specific friction that drives the workshop-avoidant buyer; H5 closes on the primary emotional benefit of membership: set-and-forget, nothing to manage, done once

**Why "Book Once. We Handle the Rest." matters here:** The membership buyer's core anxiety isn't about quality — it's about logistics. They don't fear a bad detail; they fear having to remember to rebook every month. "Book Once. We Handle the Rest." is a direct promise that the management burden goes away. It's the emotional close for the most common reason Melbourne professionals have for not getting regular car care.

## Keyword Recommendations

### Exact Match — 5 Keywords

```
[car detailing membership Melbourne]
[mobile car detailing subscription Melbourne]
[monthly car detail Melbourne]
[car maintenance plan Melbourne]
[monthly detailing Toorak]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[car detailing membership Melbourne]` — primary intent term; buyer is looking for an ongoing plan, not a one-off booking; this is the highest-intent membership query in the Melbourne market and Pristine must own it
- `[mobile car detailing subscription Melbourne]` — subscription framing; buyer has already made the conceptual leap from "booking a detail" to "paying monthly for regular service"; this buyer is closer to converting than any other segment and will have a very high close rate if the landing page confirms the product model clearly
- `[monthly car detail Melbourne]` — slightly broader intent; buyer may not know the word "membership" but is searching for the outcome (regular monthly service); this term captures the consideration stage buyer before they have decided on membership vs. repeat one-off booking
- `[car maintenance plan Melbourne]` — vehicle-care framing; buyers who use the word "plan" are thinking in terms of structured ongoing care, not reactive single services; often overlaps with the ICP who is also researching service intervals and paint maintenance together
- `[monthly detailing Toorak]` — suburb-level intent in Pristine's highest-value postcode; a Toorak resident searching for monthly detailing is not price-sensitive and is already bought into the concept; this term typically yields the highest Average Order Value leads in the membership funnel

---

### Negative Keywords — 3 to Exclude

```
[car wash subscription]
[DIY car detailing kit]
[waterless car wash]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[car wash subscription]` — captures drive-through wash service buyers (Midas, Benny's, subscription tunnel wash models); completely different product, different price expectation ($15–$30/month), different quality standard; these buyers will click and immediately bounce when they see $99/mo for a professional mobile service
- `[DIY car detailing kit]` — self-install intent; buyer is equipping themselves to detail the car, not hiring a professional; opposite of Pristine's ICP; will consume ad spend and provide zero pipeline value
- `[waterless car wash]` — budget segment looking for spray-and-wipe products; signals a buyer who does not value professional application or the protection outcomes Pristine provides; self-filters for price, not quality

## Retargeting Note

For users who visited `/membership` or `/booking` with a membership service selected but did not convert:

> **Headline:** Your Membership Is One Click Away
> **Description:** Essential from $99/mo. We come to you monthly in Toorak, Brighton, Hawthorn. No contracts. Book now.

Window: 7-day hot retargeting.
Frequency cap: 2 impressions/week.

Message angle: The membership buyer who reached the booking page and left is almost certainly still interested — the product is right, the price is known, and the mechanics are clear. The retargeting message restates the key logistics reassurance (no contracts, mobile, local suburb names) and lowers the action threshold by framing the click as completing something already started. "Your Membership Is One Click Away" treats the prior visit as partial progress, not a cold start — this framing converts better than discount offers or urgency claims for this price point.

## Context Notes

- **Why Membership in week 31:** The July cold-weather window creates the "I've been meaning to sort this" psychological moment — winter maintenance anxiety peaks when the car looks worse than it did before winter. Membership reframes the problem: instead of "I need to book a detail," the message becomes "I need to never think about this again."
- **Melbourne-specific hooks used:** Toorak, Brighton, Hawthorn named in keywords and descriptions; "home or office" resonates with Inner East professionals who cannot easily access a workshop in industrial Melbourne East
- **Tone check:** No superlatives, no "passionate about cars," no "best in Melbourne." "Set-and-forget," "we come to you," "book once" — these are the customer's own language (from product-marketing-context.md verbatim)
- **Membership vs. one-off ads:** This campaign does NOT promote one-off services. The landing page and retargeting message stay on membership. Cross-promoting ceramic or PPF in the same ad fragments intent and reduces quality score.
