## Campaign Context

**Focus service:** Paint Correction
**Why now:** Week 40 of the rotation. Melbourne spring is underway — car owners coming out of winter are noticing swirl marks, bird-dropping etch, and oxidation they've been ignoring. **Audience intent:** High. People searching "paint correction Melbourne" have already researched the service. They're comparing studios, not learning what paint correction is.

## Strategy Notes

### Bid strategy
- Start on **Maximise Clicks** with a CPC cap of $3.50 (paint correction is a high-ticket service; qualified clicks are worth more than average local service)
- After 30–50 conversions, shift to **Target CPA** based on observed booking rate

### Geo targeting
- **Primary:** Postcode radius — Toorak (3142), South Yarra (3141), Brighton (3186), Hawthorn (3122), Richmond (3121), Bayside suburbs (Sandringham 3191, Hampton 3188, Black Rock 3193)
- **Secondary:** Mornington Peninsula (seasonal — spring/summer car show season)
- **Exclude:** Western suburbs (Sunshine, Werribee) — low ICP match by demographic data

### Ad schedule
- Run: Mon–Sat, 7am–8pm AEST
- Peak spend: Thu–Sat (weekend booking intent highest)
- Reduce bids Sunday morning (lower intent on Sunday; save budget for Mon)

### Upsell flag
- In ad extensions (Sitelinks), add "Add Ceramic Coating" and "Add Glass Coating" links — paint correction customers are highest-conversion ceramic coating prospects.
