## What Is Mobile Window Tinting?

## What's Legal in Victoria?

Victoria has specific VLT (Visible Light Transmission) requirements. VLT measures what percentage of light passes through the glass — the lower the number, the darker the tint.

| Window | Minimum VLT Required |
|--------|---------------------|
| Front windscreen | No tint below the AS/NZS 2080 line |
| Front side windows (driver and passenger) | ≥35% VLT |
| Rear side windows | No restriction — any darkness allowed |
| Rear windscreen | No restriction — any darkness allowed |

The 35% front window rule is strictly enforced at roadworthiness inspections. Most Melbourne drivers choose 35% VLT for the fronts — barely noticeable in normal light — and go darker on rear windows, typically 15–20%, for cabin privacy.

Any tinter who tells you they can put 20% VLT on your front side windows without legal risk is wrong. Don't take that advice.

## How Mobile Window Tinting Works

No workshop needed. Here's how the process runs:

1. **Booking** — Tell us your vehicle make, model, and which windows you want tinted. We confirm timing and location.
2. **Setup** — The technician arrives with pre-cut film templates and sets up a dust-controlled area around your car.
3. **Glass prep** — Every window is cleaned and decontaminated before any film goes on. Adhesion fails on dirty glass.
4. **Film application** — Film is applied wet, squeegeed flat, and trimmed to edge tolerance. Modern films bond chemically — no peeling once cured.
5. **Cure time** — Films need 2–5 days to fully cure (longer in cooler weather). Don't wind down the windows during this period.
6. **Inspection** — Before the technician leaves, every panel is checked for bubbles or edge lifts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does window tinting take?**
A standard four-door sedan takes 1.5–2.5 hours. SUVs typically run 2–3 hours. We give you a confirmed time estimate at booking.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. We've completed installs in apartment car parks in South Yarra, Prahran, and the CBD. The technician sets up portable lighting and a clean work area. You'll need enough clearance for a full door swing on both sides.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Window tinting is an exterior modification and doesn't affect any mechanical or electrical warranty. The exception would be if your manufacturer explicitly specifies otherwise — rare, but worth confirming with your dealer before you book.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
A minimum of 35% VLT is required for both front side windows. The windscreen also has restrictions on where film can be applied. No restriction applies to rear side windows or the rear windscreen.

**What about factory-tinted glass — does that count?**
Factory tint counts toward your VLT reading. If your rear windows came from the factory at 25% VLT and you add aftermarket film on top, the combined reading is what matters for compliance — not the aftermarket film alone.
