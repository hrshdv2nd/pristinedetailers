## Why Window Tinting Matters in Melbourne

Melbourne's UV index regularly peaks at 9–10 in summer — among the highest in the world for a major city. Untinted glass lets UV straight through, fading leather, cracking dashboards, and driving cabin temperatures up by 20°C on a hot day. Quality window film blocks 99% of UV and reduces solar heat build-up significantly.

There's a practical security angle too. Tinted rear windows make valuables less visible in car parks — relevant if you park regularly at Crown Casino, Westfield South Yarra, or underground in the CBD.

## Victoria's Legal Tint Limits

VicRoads sets strict rules on how dark your windows can be. The technical measure is Visible Light Transmission (VLT): the percentage of visible light that passes through the glass. Lower VLT equals darker tint.

| Window | Minimum Legal VLT |
|--------|------------------|
| Windscreen | No tint permitted (OEM sun strip at top only) |
| Front driver's side window | ≥35% VLT |
| Front passenger's side window | ≥35% VLT |
| Rear passenger windows | No minimum — any tint permitted |
| Rear window | No minimum — any tint permitted |

The practical read: you can go as dark as you want on rear windows. Front side windows must let at least 35% of light through — a medium tint that looks clean but isn't excessively dark. We only fit VicRoads-compliant film on front windows.

## How Mobile Window Tinting Works

No workshop. No drop-off at a factory unit in Moorabbin. Here's what the process looks like:

1. **Book your appointment** — choose a time and location (driveway, visitor car park, or office)
2. **Technician arrives with everything** — film rolls, cutting tools, application solution, lint-free setup
3. **Glass is cleaned** with isopropyl alcohol and a clay bar to remove contamination — film adhesion depends on a clean surface
4. **Film is cut precisely** to your vehicle's glass dimensions, either from our pre-cut database or trimmed on-site
5. **Application** — film is laid with a slip solution that allows repositioning before it bonds to the glass
6. **Drying and edge inspection** — the technician checks for bubbles, lifted edges, and coverage before leaving
7. **Cure period: 3–5 days** before winding windows down fully

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does window tinting take?**
Most sedans take 2–3 hours. SUVs and vehicles with panoramic sunroofs typically take 3–4 hours. Exact time is confirmed when you book.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. We work in underground car parks regularly — the covered environment is actually preferable, as direct sun causes the application solution to evaporate too quickly. Adequate lighting is the only real requirement.

**Does window tinting void my car's warranty?**
No. Window film is a non-invasive surface application and does not affect your vehicle warranty under Australian Consumer Law. No glass, seals, or electrical components are modified.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
Front side windows must have a minimum VLT of 35% — meaning 35% of visible light must pass through the glass. The windscreen cannot be tinted except for an OEM sun strip at the top. Rear windows have no VLT minimum under Victorian law.
