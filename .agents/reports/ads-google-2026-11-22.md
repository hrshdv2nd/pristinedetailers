## Seasonal Context

Week 47 sits at the intersection of three live buying signals that are relevant for Membership search intent:

1. **Christmas gifting window** — Five weeks to Christmas Day. Searches for "car protection plan Melbourne" and similar in this window include gift-intent buyers researching on behalf of a partner or family member who owns a prestige car. The gift-buyer angle is unusual in Membership search results; most competitors run only owner-intent copy. A headline that names the gift frame will carry a higher CTR for this segment than generic maintenance copy.

2. **Warranty maintenance urgency** — Ceramic and graphene coating customers who had work done 12–18 months ago are overdue for their first annual maintenance visit. These buyers search "ceramic coating maintenance Melbourne" and "coating top up Melbourne" with high conversion intent. They are not researching the product category; they already know the obligation. The copy must confirm that Pristine handles the scheduling and that the warranty standard is maintained.

3. **Summer pre-treatment** — Melbourne summer begins in earnest in December. Buyers preparing their car for the summer season and Peninsula weekends search in late November. "Book before December" is factual studio capacity language, not manufactured urgency. The studio's December calendar genuinely fills from both Membership starts and one-off pre-summer service bookings.

All three segments map to distinct search terms and can be matched to dedicated headlines within a single RSA. Rotate the ad set across the five headlines below; Google's machine learning will weight the combinations that convert most efficiently per segment over time.

## Ad Group Structure Recommendation

For this RSA, run **two ad groups** within the same campaign:

**Ad Group 1 — Existing Coating Customers (Warranty Maintenance)**
- Keywords: [ceramic coating maintenance Melbourne], [graphene coating maintenance Melbourne], [coating maintenance plan Melbourne]
- Pin H1 ("Keep Your Warranty Active") to Position 1
- Pin D1 (warranty description) to Description Position 1
- Audience overlay: Customer match list (confirmed Pristine ceramic and graphene coating customers) — bid modifier +40%
- Bidding: Target CPA, with enhanced CPC as fallback if conversion data is thin

**Ad Group 2 — New Membership Buyers (Acquisition)**
- Keywords: [annual car protection plan Melbourne], [ceramic coating membership Melbourne]
- Leave headlines unpinned — allow machine learning to discover best combinations
- Pin H2 ("Melbourne Coating Membership") to Position 1 as a safeguard for brand relevance
- Pin D2 (Melbourne urgency description) to Description Position 1
- Bidding: Maximise Clicks until 30 conversions, then switch to Target CPA

**Campaign-level audience:** Add remarketing lists as observation layers (not targeting-only). Segments to observe: (a) Membership service page visitors — last 30 days; (b) confirmed past coating service customers. Use the data to validate that the in-market warranty maintenance searchers (Ad Group 1) are converting at a lower CPA than new acquisition buyers (Ad Group 2). If confirmed, shift 60% of the campaign budget to Ad Group 1 by the end of week 1.
