## Article Body

Prestige cars require more than a standard detail. BMW's thin factory lacquer, Porsche's matte paint options, Range Rover's dealership-inflicted swirl patterns, and Tesla's delivery-day panel inconsistencies each demand a specific prep and coating stack — and getting it wrong damages paint that's expensive to fix.

![Sports car front panel ready for protection](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1503376780353-7e6692767b70?w=1200&q=80)

## BMW: The Thinnest Clear Coat on the Market

BMW uses one of the softest, thinnest lacquer clear coats of any production car. It's a trade-off the factory makes for paint depth and gloss, but it means swirl marks appear faster than most owners realise. A single pass through an automatic brush wash can leave visible scratches under direct sunlight.

Before any coating goes on, a two-stage machine polish is typically required. Stage one removes existing damage; stage two refines the finish so the nano-ceramic bonds to a clean, corrected surface. Skipping correction and coating over swirls locks them in permanently — under a 5-year coating.

Once corrected, BMW's soft lacquer actually benefits more from a ceramic coating than harder paints. The nano-ceramic adds chemical resistance the factory clear coat was never designed to provide on its own.

## Porsche: Matte and Satin Paint Require a Different Product Stack

Standard Porsche models in solid or metallic paint behave similarly to other European brands. The issue arises with GT and RS variants finished in Porsche's satin or matte options. These aren't topcoat finishes applied over standard paint — they're a different clear coat formulation entirely, and they're not compatible with conventional ceramic products.

Applying a standard ceramic coating to Porsche matte paint will alter the surface sheen permanently. The correct stack uses matte-specific ceramic formulations engineered to protect without changing the look.

If you own a GT3, GT4, or any RS variant in a matte or satin finish, ask any studio one question before booking: "Do you stock a matte-safe ceramic product?" If they hesitate, the answer is no.

> "The finish on my GT3 is better than factory. Water rolls off in sheets. Worth every dollar." — Marcus T., 2024 Porsche 911 GT3

## Range Rover: What the Dealership Has Already Done to Your Paint

Land Rover's authorised service program uses rotary brush wash equipment at most dealerships. Rotary brushes on soft Land Rover paint reliably produce characteristic swirl patterns — the kind visible as grey haze in direct sun and particularly obvious on darker finishes: Carpathian Grey, Santorini Black, Narvik Black.

Most Range Rovers arriving at our studio carry the same damage: spiralling swirl marks across the bonnet, roof, and boot lid — exactly where horizontal surfaces contact the brush under pressure during dealer services.

Correction comes first. After polishing out the damage, a ceramic or graphene coating seals the surface against subsequent car park contact and poorly handled washes. For daily drivers in Melbourne's inner east, PPF on the bonnet and front bumper alongside the coating addresses stone chip risk from the Eastern Freeway and Eastlink.

## Tesla: Factory Issues Before You've Driven a Kilometre

Tesla's manufacturing has improved, but paint quality on delivery day still varies more than any other brand at comparable price points. Panel gaps, minor surface contamination, and texture inconsistencies on new deliveries are common enough that paint correction before coating is standard practice for most Tesla customers.

The specific issue: soft clear coat combined with inadequate transport protection. By the time a car reaches Melbourne through the distribution chain, the paint has often picked up contamination that requires chemical decontamination and machine polishing before a coating can bond correctly.

Graphene coating is the most common choice for Teslas. Superior heat dissipation matters for a car that sits in direct Melbourne UV while charging, and the 9-year manufacturer warranty aligns with how long most Tesla owners hold their cars.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does a ceramic coating void my prestige car warranty?**
No. Ceramic and graphene coatings are applied over your clear coat, not to mechanical components. No Australian prestige car manufacturer voids their warranty based on a paint coating.

**Do you come to my home in Toorak?**
Coating and PPF work is done at our Melbourne studio. The lighting, booth conditions, and equipment required for correction and coating can't be replicated in a home garage or showroom. Mobile window tinting is the one service we bring to the customer.

**Can you work on Porsche matte paint?**
Yes. We stock matte-safe ceramic formulations for GT and RS variants. Not all studios carry them — confirm before booking anywhere.

**My Tesla had paint defects on delivery. Can you fix them?**
Yes. Pre-coating paint correction is standard on new Teslas at our studio. A two-stage correction removes delivery contamination and surface defects before the ceramic or graphene coating is applied.
