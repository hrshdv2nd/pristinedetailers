**Recommended bid strategy:** Manual CPC to start (conversion data is thin on PPF high-ticket); move to Target CPA after 20+ conversions.

**Landing page guidance:** PPF-specific page should address the "will it scratch my car?" anxiety and show before/after on a Porsche or Range Rover — our ICP's cars. CTA should be "Get a PPF Quote" not a generic "Book Now."

**Ad schedule:** Run 7am–8pm AEST Mon–Sat; suppress Sundays initially and review after 30 days.

**Geo targeting:** Inner East (Toorak, Hawthorn, Camberwell, Richmond) and Bayside (Brighton, Sandringham, Mentone, Mornington Peninsula). Exclude postcode areas below $90K household income threshold.
