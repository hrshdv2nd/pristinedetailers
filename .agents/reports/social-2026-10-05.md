**Caption:**

Before a ceramic or graphene coating is applied, the paint underneath it has to be right. This is the step that separates a certified studio from a quick-coat installer.

Ceramic and graphene coatings create a permanent nano-bond with the surface they're applied to. If that surface has swirl marks, fine scratches, or oxidation — and most vehicles do after even 6–12 months on Melbourne roads — those defects are sealed in for the life of the coating. They don't disappear. They get locked in.

At Pristine Detailers, paint correction is assessed on every job. Where the surface needs work, we do a machine polish first. It adds time, but it's what produces a finish that genuinely looks better than factory — and stays that way for up to 9 years under a graphene coating.

For business owners and fleet managers considering protection for company or prestige vehicles: the question to ask your installer is "what prep work is included?" If the answer is just a wash and decontamination, you're likely getting coating applied over imperfections. Worth knowing before you sign off.
