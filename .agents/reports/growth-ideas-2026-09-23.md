## 5 Growth Ideas

### Idea 1 — "New Car, First Drive" Short-Form Video Series
**Category:** Social & Content (#35–44)  
**Effort:** Low | **Impact:** High

**What it is:** Film every high-value car that arrives for PPF or ceramic coating before its first real drive. Publish as Instagram Reels and TikToks with the hook: "Before this [car] sees a single Melbourne pothole, this is what we do." Focus on process, product application, and the before/after — not the brand.

**Why it fits:** New car content generates some of the highest organic reach in the automotive niche. Car owners already searching for "ceramic coating Melbourne" are on the same platforms. Each video is both a trust signal and a proof point: the types of cars in the content tell viewers exactly who Pristine serves. The studio produces the content as a by-product of the job — no separate shoot required.

**Expected outcome:** 5–10 Reels/week that compound into an organic audience of in-market Melbourne car owners. First 3 months builds the algorithm; months 4–6 generates inbound DM enquiries without ad spend.

**Resources:** One phone mount, decent lighting already in studio, 30 min/week editing. Zero budget.

**What it is:** Partner with 3–4 Melbourne luxury and performance car clubs — Porsche Club Victoria, Ferrari Club of Victoria, BMW Car Club Australia (Vic), Tesla Owners Club Melbourne. Club members talk constantly about who did their paint work. One trusted referral from the club president is worth 10 Google Ads clicks. The investment is one free coating job (~$750–$999 COGS on a ceramic/graphene) that buys access to 200–1,000 pre-qualified leads.

**Expected outcome:** 2–5 new bookings per month per club at full ticket price. High close rate because the referral comes with social proof baked in. Members who book become club ambassadors who continue the loop.

**Resources:** 1–2 complimentary coating jobs as entry cost. Outreach email to club committee. One printed one-pager for club welcome packs.

### Idea 3 — Prestige Car Dealership Partnership
**Category:** Partnerships (#54–64)  
**Effort:** Medium | **Impact:** High

**What it is:** Approach 5–8 prestige car dealerships in the Inner East (Zagame Automotive, CMG, Inchcape, Barbagallo, Trivett) and position Pristine as the day-one protection specialist they recommend to every new car buyer. **Resources:** Partnership deck, sample leave-behind card, referral tracking method (simple promo code), 2–3 in-person meetings.

### Idea 4 — "Should I Get PPF or Ceramic?" Interactive Quiz / Lead Capture
**Category:** Free Tools & Lead Magnets (#14–22)  
**Effort:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium-High

**What it is:** A simple 5-question quiz on the Pristine website: car make, age, primary concern (stone chips vs. shine vs. UV), budget range, and suburb. Output: a personalised recommendation ("Based on your Porsche 911 in Toorak, here's what we'd do") plus an estimated price range and a booking CTA. Email is captured before the result is shown.

**Why it fits:** "PPF vs ceramic vs graphene" is the #1 decision-stage question every potential customer has. Most competitors make people call for a quote. A quiz answers the question instantly, captures the lead, and positions Pristine as the expert before any conversation starts. The email goes into an automated nurture sequence.

**Effort:** Medium — requires building the quiz (Typeform or a custom Webflow component), writing the result logic, and wiring to email automation.

**Expected outcome:** 30–80 qualified email leads/month from organic and paid traffic. Conversion rate from quiz completion to booking estimated at 15–25% with a good follow-up sequence.

**Resources:** Typeform or similar ($0–$50/month), 1–2 days to build, email automation setup (connects to Week 2 email sequence work).

### Idea 5 — Bayside & Inner East Direct Mail Drop
**Category:** Guerrilla / Direct Mail (#121)  
**Effort:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium

**What it is:** A precision physical mail campaign to households in Toorak (3142), South Yarra (3141), Brighton (3186), Hawthorn (3122), and Bayside suburbs — targeting high-income postcodes with known concentrations of premium car ownership. Not a flyer — a premium matte card stock piece with a single strong headline, one testimonial, and a QR code to book.

A physical, high-quality piece signals premium brand and cuts through. The offer should be specific: "This month only — complimentary Glass Coating ($150 value) with any ceramic or graphene coating booked before [date]." Urgency + tangibility.

**Effort:** Medium — requires Australia Post's Unaddressed Mail service (or targeted letterbox drops using suburb lists), copywriting, and design. Budget: ~$0.80–$1.20 per piece. Recommend 2,000–3,000 pieces for a test run ($1,600–$3,600).

**Expected outcome:** 0.5–2% response rate = 10–60 enquiries from one drop. Even 5 booked ceramic jobs at $750 = $3,750 gross from a $2,000 campaign. Test one suburb before scaling.

**Resources:** Design asset (reuse existing brand assets), Australia Post lodgement, budget ~$2,000–$4,000 for a test run.

## Top 2 Execution Briefs

### Brief 1 — "New Car, First Drive" Short-Form Video Series

The execution is simple because the content already exists in the studio every day. Set up a fixed phone mount (or a $150 magnetic clamp mount) on one wall of the studio, pointing at the prep area. When a car arrives for a coating or PPF job, shoot 30–60 seconds of: (1) the car arriving, (2) prep in action, (3) the coating or film going on, (4) the finished result with water beading. No voiceover needed — captions do the work. The caption formula is: "This [Year] [Car] came in before its first Melbourne drive. Here's the protection we put on it." Post to Instagram Reels first, then cross-post to TikTok. Volume matters more than polish — 4–5 posts per week compounds the algorithm faster than 1 polished video per week. In the first 4 weeks: post 20 videos, review which car/service type generated the most saves and shares, then double down on that format. The goal in month 1 is not bookings — it's finding the 2–3 video formats that the Melbourne car audience consistently saves and shares. Bookings follow at month 3–4 as the account gains algorithmic trust and inbound enquiry starts replacing cold search.

### Brief 2 — Car Club Ambassador Program

Start with Porsche Club Victoria — it has the highest density of Pristine's ICP and a well-run committee that manages sponsorship enquiries professionally. The outreach pitch is not "can we advertise in your newsletter" — it's "we'd like to do a complimentary full graphene coating on the club president's car, so they can give members a first-hand account." That framing changes the dynamic from vendor to trusted expert. Once the job is done, ask for a single post in the club's member communication (Facebook group or newsletter) from the president about the result — not a sponsored post, a genuine experience post. This single post, in a community of 400–800 members who own exactly the right cars, is worth more than a year of Instagram ads. After Porsche Club Victoria, replicate for BMW Car Club Australia (Vic) and Ferrari Club of Victoria. Track all bookings that come in mentioning the club and calculate the CAC per club partnership. At an average ticket of $999 for graphene coating and an estimated 3–6 bookings per club per month, the ROI on 1–2 complimentary jobs as the entry cost is clear within 30 days.

- Video series → generates awareness → viewer becomes enquiry → enquiry becomes a booking
- Car club ambassador → member becomes customer → customer's result is the next video → car club mates see the video → more bookings

The strongest loop: **Video series + Car Club** feeds itself. The car club member's car becomes content. The content reaches more car club members. No paid media required to sustain it.
