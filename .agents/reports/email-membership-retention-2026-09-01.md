# Email Sequence: Membership Retention
**Focus:** Re-engage members who haven't booked in 45+ days  
**Date written:** 2026-09-01  
**Melbourne season:** Early Spring (September)  
**Skill used:** churn-prevention  

---

## Context

These three emails target active members on a Pristine Detailers subscription who haven't booked in 45+ days. They're still being charged. They're not using it. The goal is to pull them back before they question why they're paying for something they're not booking.

September is the first month of Melbourne's spring. The city's coming out of its coldest months, and cars are carrying the accumulated load of winter — road grime, iron fallout, oxidation building slowly under the surface. But spring brings its own challenges: wattle and paperbark pollen is settling (mildly acidic on clear coat), magpies are in swooping and nesting season (bird droppings are up), and people are driving more as the weather opens up. These aren't invented reasons to book — they're accurate to right now, specific to Melbourne, and relevant to the kind of cars our members drive.

This sequence uses spring as the frame without leaning on it too hard. Email 1 checks in. Email 2 makes unused membership value concrete and ties it to a real spring maintenance case. Email 3 offers something genuinely useful for the season.

Suppress the sequence if a booking is made at any point. Escalate to a human if Email 3 triggers a reply about cancelling or pausing.

Save offer this month: **free exterior glass sealant treatment** (approx. $75 value). Distinct from the July offer (iron decontamination) and the June offer (interior detail add-on). Spring rain, bird activity, and increased driving make it credible and timely.

## Seasonal Note — Early Spring Frame (September)

September in Melbourne is the opening of spring: wattle pollen settling on paintwork, magpies active in nesting/swooping season (bird droppings increase meaningfully), and cars emerging from winter carrying accumulated fallout and surface contamination. People are driving more. The UV is building toward the harsh summer levels that Melbourne is known for. The key distinction from winter emails: spring's urgency isn't about damage accumulating in the cold — it's about resetting before the warm season and getting ahead of UV, which is the thing most members with ceramic coatings genuinely care about. The glass sealant offer is spring-specific and technically credible, and it's different enough from the last two save offers that members who received those sequences won't read it as a template.
