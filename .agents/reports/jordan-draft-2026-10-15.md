## What the Law Says: Victorian VLT Limits

VLT stands for Visible Light Transmission — the percentage of light that passes through the film. Higher VLT means lighter tint. Lower VLT means darker.

Under Victorian road rules:

| Window | Minimum VLT | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Windscreen (above AS/NZS line) | 35% | Applies to the visor strip only |
| Front side windows | 35% | Applies to driver and front passenger |
| Rear side windows | No minimum | Can be as dark as 5% VLT |
| Rear windscreen | No minimum | Can be as dark as 5% VLT |

Most customers go 35% VLT on the front for legal compliance and 20% or 5% on the rear for privacy and heat rejection. We can advise on what suits your vehicle and use case before we start.

## How It Works: Mobile Installation Process

1. **Booking** — choose your coverage level (full vehicle or partial), confirm your address and parking situation. We need a covered or shaded area and about 3 metres of working space around the car.
2. **Surface prep** — windows are cleaned and decontaminated to remove any residue that would cause bubbles or lifting.
3. **Film cut** — patterns are precision-cut to your specific vehicle's glass shapes using digital templates for accuracy.
4. **Installation** — film is applied wet and squeegeed into position. Air pockets are worked out before the adhesive sets.
5. **Cure time** — the film needs 3–5 days to fully bond. During this period, avoid winding down windows.

A full-vehicle install takes 3–4 hours. Rear glass only takes 60–90 minutes.

## FAQ

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
Front side windows must be 35% VLT or higher. The windscreen can have a tint strip above the AS/NZS line. Anything darker than 35% on the front is not road-legal and will fail a roadworthy.

**How long does window tinting take?**
Full vehicle: 3–4 hours. Rear windows only: 60–90 minutes. You can wait nearby or we can lock up when finished if you need to be elsewhere.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. We do it regularly across South Yarra, Richmond, and CBD apartment buildings. We need reasonable lighting, about 3 metres of working clearance around the car, and confirmation from your building management if required by your strata.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Window film applied externally to the glass does not affect any manufacturer warranty on your vehicle's mechanical components. Some OEM glass warranties may have conditions — check your vehicle handbook if you're unsure.

**What if it rains on installation day?**
We install indoors or undercover. Rain doesn't affect the process as long as we have a sheltered space to work. If you're booking a driveway install and Melbourne weather is typical, we'll confirm a covered option or reschedule.
