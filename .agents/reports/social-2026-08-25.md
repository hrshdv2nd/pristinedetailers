## Platform: LinkedIn

**Caption:**

Most professionals washing their vehicle weekly at an automatic car wash believe they're maintaining it. The mechanics of what's actually happening are different.

Automated car wash brushes introduce swirl marks into clear coat across every horizontal surface. Not after one visit — after 36 months of regular use, the bonnet of a 2024 Range Rover Sport presented fully loaded with micro-scratches that scattered light instead of reflecting it. Pair that with Melbourne's urban bird activity and you add acid etch marks from droppings that weren't removed within 48 hours: pH 3.5 sitting on clear coat bites, and it does so faster in winter when contact time is extended by rain.

The fix: two-stage machine paint correction — a cut stage to remove the compromised layer and a refine stage to restore gloss — followed by a 5-year ceramic coating on corrected, clean paint. The owner's question at the end: "Did you do anything to the panels?" We didn't. We removed three years of accumulated damage from them.
