## Email 1: Post-Booking Confirmation (Updated — Summer Context)

**Trigger:** Immediately on booking confirmation
**Goal:** Set professional expectations, reduce pre-job anxiety, differentiate from the moment of booking
**What's new this iteration:** Added summer-specific paint care warning and adjusted timing language

---

**Subject:** Booking confirmed — a few things before [Appointment Date]
**Preview:** The prep your coating needs before we start. Takes 5 minutes to read.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Your [Service] is confirmed for [Appointment Date]. Here's exactly what happens before we touch your car.

**Before you bring it in**

Wash the car 24–48 hours before your appointment if you can. Don't apply any wax, spray detailer, or product to the panels beforehand — it creates a barrier that affects coating adhesion and adds prep time on our end.

If you have fresh stone chips, known swirl marks, or surface scratches you want us to address, reply to this email now. It's easier to plan the correction before the job than to discover it on the day.

One thing worth knowing in the current heat: **fresh bird droppings should be removed before you arrive.** The uric acid in bird droppings becomes much more aggressive on paint in summer temperatures — left for even a few hours, it can etch through clear coat. If there are any on the car, remove them with a damp cloth before bringing it in.

**On the day**

Your technician will begin with a full decontamination wash — not a rinse, a true decon wash that removes bonded contamination most studios skip. From there:

- **Ceramic or Graphene:** Panel inspection → polish (if included in your package) → coating application in a temperature-controlled environment → initial cure
- **PPF:** Panel prep → film application → edge wrap → squeegee and heat cure
- **Window tinting:** Film cut and application → squeegee out → cure time

**Collection**

You'll get an SMS when the car is ready. For coatings: keep the car dry for 12–24 hours — no rain, no wash, no wet garage. Full care instructions are on the day.

Any questions before your appointment: reply here. These go directly to your technician.

See you on [Appointment Date].

[Technician Name]

## Email 3: 30-Day Dormant Win-Back (Updated — Summer Urgency)

**Trigger:** 30 days since last completed booking, no new booking in CRM
**Goal:** Reactivate with genuine maintenance value; summer hook makes the timing feel natural, not pushy
**What's new this iteration:** Previous version mentioned "before Melbourne summer" as a future event — this version delivers it as present reality

---

**Subject:** [First Name], your coating at 30 days — before summer really hits
**Preview:** One maintenance step most people skip. Worth 2 minutes.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

It's been about a month since your [Service] at Pristine. Melbourne's summer is arriving — and that's worth a quick note.

**Your coating is working.** Stone chips, UV, bird droppings — they're hitting the coating surface, not your paint. But a few things can quietly shorten a coating's working life over summer if left unchecked.

**What to watch for over the next 90 days:**

**Bird droppings and tree sap — remove within 4 hours in summer.**
On a hot day, bird dropping uric acid becomes significantly more corrosive on paint and coatings. The rule is simple: if you see it, deal with it now.

**Automated car washes — avoid them.**
The rotating brushes micro-scratch the coating surface and reduce its hydrophobic performance over time. Hand wash with a clean microfibre mitt, or a touchless wash only.

**Iron contamination on your wheels.**
Brake dust bonds to coating surfaces over summer in Melbourne traffic. An iron decontamination spray every 3–6 months keeps wheels clean without stripping the coating. Reply and we'll recommend what to use.

**Annual maintenance coating — if you're approaching 12 months:**
Most Ceramic Pro and Gtechniq warranties require an annual maintenance coating to remain valid. It's a 2–3 hour studio visit and keeps your warranty intact. If your first coat was in spring or summer last year, now is the right time to book before summer slots fill.

If everything is still performing, good — that's exactly what it should be doing.

Two jobs with us. That means you're already in a small group of Melbourne car owners who've taken paint protection seriously — most people talk about it, fewer actually do it.

Since you've been in twice, I want to give you an honest answer to the question we hear most often from customers at this stage: **"What would you actually do next?"**

---

**If you're on ceramic coating:**

Graphene is the next meaningful step — and I'll be specific about why it matters in Melbourne.

Melbourne summers are hot. Graphene coating dissipates heat from the paint surface more efficiently than ceramic — this reduces heat-induced chemical bonding of contaminants (bird droppings, iron particles) to the surface, which is relevant from November through February. The molecular bond is also denser than ceramic, giving better scratch resistance and a more hydrophobic surface that stays cleaner between washes.

This isn't a marginal upgrade. Graphene coating from **$999**. Your paint prep history is already on file — we know your car's surface.

---

**If you haven't added PPF yet:**

Ceramic and graphene handle UV, water, and chemical contamination. What they don't stop is physical impact — stone chips on the freeway, car park door dings, gravel on the Mornington Peninsula.

PPF is the physical barrier that handles all of that. The combination — **PPF on vulnerable panels, then graphene coating over the top** — means your paint is covered from every direction.

We do this combination regularly. The installs are sequenced correctly so the film and coating bond properly. PPF from **$3,000**.

## Email 5: Pre-Summer Campaign (New — Not in September Report)

**Trigger:** Proactive campaign send to full customer list (past customers who haven't booked in 90+ days) in November
**Goal:** Generate bookings before summer slot competition increases; create urgency without manufacturing it
**Type:** Campaign / seasonal re-engagement
**Why now:** November is the last window before Melbourne's December–January peak. Customers who care about their car's protection have a genuine reason to act now.

---

**Subject:** Melbourne summer is two weeks out — is your paint ready?
**Preview:** A short checklist from six years of watching paint take a beating in December.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Melbourne summer starts in earnest in the next two weeks. After six years of seeing what this city does to paint between November and February, here's what we'd check on any car worth protecting:

**Is your coating still performing?**
Run water over your car's bonnet. If it sheets off in droplets, the hydrophobic layer is working. If it sheets flat (no beading), the coating is degraded and your paint is exposed to UV and chemical contamination without protection.

**When did you last have a maintenance coating?**
Ceramic Pro and Gtechniq warranties require an annual maintenance coating to stay valid. If it's been more than 12 months since your last service with us, the warranty clock has started. A maintenance coat takes 2–3 hours and keeps everything valid.

**Have you been using an automated car wash?**
If yes: the rotating brushes have almost certainly introduced micro-scratches into the coating surface. This is worth assessing — a light machine polish and re-coat can restore hydrophobic performance significantly.

**Do your vulnerable panels have PPF?**
Ceramic and graphene protect against UV and chemical load. They don't stop stone chips on the Monash or the South Eastern. If your front bumper, bonnet, or door edges don't have film, Melbourne freeway driving in summer is the highest-risk period.

---

If your car passed all four — good, it's ready.

If any of these flagged something worth looking at: we have availability in November. December books out quickly.

[→ Book a summer assessment]
