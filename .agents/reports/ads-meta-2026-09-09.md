## Targeting

### Cold Audience (Prospecting)

**Location:**
- Melbourne metro
- Target postcodes with enhanced interest bid: Toorak (3142), South Yarra (3141), Brighton (3186), Hawthorn (3122), Richmond (3121), Bayside corridor (3183–3196), Mornington Peninsula (3930–3941)

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28–55
- Gender: All (skew male, but don't exclude women — Toorak/Bayside audience is mixed)

**Interests:**
- BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Tesla, Audi, Range Rover, Ferrari (car brand pages)
- Luxury vehicles, automotive, car care, high-performance cars
- Exclude: Automotive DIY, car cleaning products (DIY anti-persona)

**Behaviours:**
- Engaged shoppers
- Recent car purchase (3–6 months) if available via Meta signals
- High household income bracket

### Lookalike Audience
- Source: Existing customer list (2,400+ cars — upload email/phone data)

## Campaign Notes

- **Objective:** Conversions (booking page submission or click-to-book)
- **Budget split:** 70% cold prospecting / 30% retargeting
- **Ad set naming:** `META_Conv_LAL1-Melb_CeramicCoating_Sep26` / `META_Conv_Retarget-Booking_CeramicCoating_Sep26`
- **UTM parameters:** `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ceramic-coating&utm_content=[variant-1|2|3]`
- **Creative rotation:** Start all 3 variants equal-weight; after 1,000 impressions each, pull the winner and iterate on the angle
- **Exclusions:** Existing customers (upload suppression list), /booking page converters (post-purchase window 30 days)
