## What Victoria's Window Tinting Laws Actually Say

Before any tint goes on, you need to know what's legal. Victoria's Road Safety (Vehicles) Regulations set specific Visible Light Transmission (VLT) limits — the percentage of light that passes through the glass. Higher VLT means a lighter tint. Lower VLT means darker.

| Window Position | Minimum VLT (Victoria) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Windscreen (front) | 75% VLT | Factory glass typically meets this; aftermarket tinting prohibited |
| Front side windows (driver + passenger) | 35% VLT | Most popular shade for Melbourne drivers |
| Rear side windows | No minimum | Can go as dark as needed |
| Rear windscreen | No minimum | Dark is legal; working rear-view mirror required |

The most common combination for Melbourne drivers is 35% VLT on front windows and 20% VLT on rear — blocking significant heat and UV without triggering a defect notice.

If your front side windows are darker than 35% VLT on a Victoria-registered vehicle, you risk a roadworthy fail and a fine. That's not a grey area.

## How Mobile Window Tinting Works

You don't drop the car at a workshop in Moorabbin and wait two days. Here's the actual process:

1. **Booking** — Choose a time and location: your home driveway in Toorak, your office car park in South Yarra, your apartment building in Richmond or Hawthorn.
2. **Arrival** — The technician arrives with a full mobile setup: tinting film, cutting tools, heat gun, and application solution.
3. **Glass prep** — Every window is cleaned thoroughly to remove contaminants that would cause bubbling or peeling under the film. This step is where most DIY jobs fail.
4. **Film cutting and application** — Film is cut to the exact glass dimensions and applied from inside the vehicle. Squeegees remove air pockets during installation.
5. **Cure time** — The film needs 3–5 days to cure fully. During this period, don't wind the windows down.

A standard four-door sedan takes 2–3 hours from setup to finish. A larger SUV typically runs 3–4 hours.

## Why Melbourne Drivers Book It

Heat is the primary driver for most enquiries. Melbourne summers push 38–42°C and the interior of a parked car regularly hits 70°C+. A quality window film cuts solar heat gain by 40–60%, which means the cabin cools down faster and your air conditioning works less.

UV protection is the other factor. The same UV that damages your car's paint also fades leather and fabric interiors over years. A window tint blocks up to 99% of UV radiation — relevant if you've invested in leather ceramic coating and want it to last.

Drivers in South Yarra, Hawthorn, and Richmond who park on the street or in multi-storey car parks notice the difference within the first summer.

## FAQ

**How long does window tinting take?**  
A standard sedan takes 2–3 hours. An SUV takes 3–4 hours. You don't need to be present the whole time.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**  
Yes — provided there's reasonable lighting for the installation. We've completed jobs in apartment car parks across South Yarra, Richmond, and Prahran. Let us know when booking if access or lighting is limited.

**Does window tinting void my car's warranty?**  
No. Window tinting is a cosmetic modification and does not affect your vehicle's mechanical warranty in Australia. Check your specific extended warranty terms if you have a third-party plan.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**  
Front side windows must allow at least 35% VLT. The windscreen cannot have aftermarket tint applied. Rear windows have no legal VLT minimum in Victoria.
