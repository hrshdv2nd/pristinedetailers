## Seasonal Context

Week 45 is the highest-intent fortnight of the year for ceramic coating acquisition. Three conditions converge right now that do not co-occur at any other point in the Melbourne calendar:

**The UV inflection window.** Melbourne's UV index moves from "moderate" (5–6) in September to "high/very high" (8–10) in November, then peaks at "extreme" (11–12) in December and January. A car owner who does not coat their paint before December is running their clear coat through the worst UV window of the Melbourne calendar completely unprotected. This is not a hypothetical risk — UV oxidation on uncoated paint is visible in 90–180 days under Melbourne summer conditions: chalk-like surface texture on dark paint, fading on light paint, and a gradual dulling of the factory finish that cannot be reversed without paint correction. The search behaviour confirms this: "ceramic coating Melbourne" searches spike between late October and early December as car owners notice their paint starting to look tired and connect the weather shift to the need to act. Week 45 is the last viable window to coat before summer peaks. The ad that communicates this urgency accurately — not as a manufactured countdown, but as a true weather reality — will convert.

**The post-Melbourne Cup / spring social season.** The Melbourne Cup ran yesterday (Tuesday 3 November). Spring Racing Carnival at Flemington has been active for three weeks. For the Inner East and Bayside ICP — Toorak, South Yarra, Brighton, Hawthorn — the spring social calendar is in full swing: Flemington enclosure appearances, Grand Prix corporate events, pre-Christmas dinner season, Peninsula weekends. A car owner attending these events is acutely aware of their car's presentation in a way they are not in winter. The transition from "the car will do for now" to "I need to sort the paint before the summer schedule begins" happens in precisely this week. The buyer searching "ceramic coating Melbourne" in the week after the Cup is not casually browsing — they are acting on an intention formed at some point in the last 30 days when they looked at their car and decided it was time.

**The spring new-car delivery cohort.** September–October is the second-highest month for new prestige car registrations in Victoria (after June/EOFY). BMW, Porsche, Range Rover, Tesla, and Mercedes-AMG deliveries peaked in September and October. These buyers took delivery 4–8 weeks ago. They have now driven their car through early spring — through the Eastern and Monash, through the Mornington Peninsula on the first warm Saturday, through Melbourne CBD car parks. The initial "it's too new to touch" hesitation is wearing off. They are becoming aware that the paint needs protection and that the optimal moment to apply a ceramic coating is before significant UV or contamination has occurred, not after. The buyer in this cohort searching for ceramic coating in Week 45 is acting on a prompt that has been building since delivery — and they are ready to book, not to research.

### Negative Keywords — 3 to Exclude

```
[ceramic coating kit]
[cheap ceramic coating]
[ceramic coating course]
```

**Rationale:**

- `[ceramic coating kit]` — DIY buyer intent. A buyer searching for a ceramic coating kit is planning to apply the product themselves. This is the anti-ICP for Pristine: DIY coating application without professional prep produces inconsistent, often worse results than no coating at all, and the kit buyer who chose Pristine's ad by mistake will bounce immediately on seeing studio pricing. The DIY buyer is also the type most likely to compare outcomes unfavourably ("it looked the same as the kit") if they do not understand what they are buying. Exclude this term entirely; it wastes spend and attracts a buyer who will not convert. This exclusion also implicitly suppresses [diy ceramic coating melbourne] and [ceramic coating diy kit] long-tail variants.

- `[cheap ceramic coating]` — price-sensitive modifier. The "cheap" qualifier is a direct anti-persona signal from the product marketing context. This buyer is optimising on price, not outcome. At $750 entry for Pristine's ceramic coating, this term will drive clicks from buyers who are expecting a $200–$300 service, produce immediate pricing page bounce, and drain the budget. Excluding "cheap" also suppresses [cheap ceramic coating melbourne], [ceramic coating under $500], and similar long-tail anti-ICP variants.

- `[ceramic coating course]` — education and training intent. A buyer searching for a ceramic coating course wants to learn how to apply coating themselves — either as a career path or as a hobbyist skill. This is a non-converting impression and click that shares the category term but represents a completely different buyer intent. The search is specifically about professional training, not professional service. Zero conversion probability. Exclude explicitly.
