## Seasonal Opportunity: August Is the Setup Month

Melbourne in August means:
- Last weeks of winter — cars are dirty from rain, road grime, wet leaves
- Owners start thinking about spring freshening before warmer weather and social season
- AFL finals season (late August) — cars are visible, people are out

The referral program should front-run this. A "spring prep" hook turns a seasonal behaviour (wanting to clean up the car) into a referral trigger. **The best time to ask a happy customer to refer someone is when they're thinking about their car anyway.**

## New Mechanic: The Community Cluster Referral

One-to-one referrals are good. Community referrals are better.

Melbourne's ICP clusters geographically and socially: apartment buildings in South Yarra, car clubs in Hawthorn, school networks in Brighton, corporate towers in the CBD. When one Pristine customer in a community refers one friend, that's one referral. But when one customer champions Pristine to their building's WhatsApp group or their car club, that's potentially 10–20 new contacts from one relationship.

### The "Street Detail" Mechanic

> **If three or more neighbours (same building or street) book in the same month, the first booker gets their next detail free.**

**How it works:**
1. Existing customer mentions Pristine to their building group chat or street neighbours
2. Three or more people book within the same calendar month — mentioning the original member's name
3. Original member earns a complimentary service at next visit (cap: Maintenance Detail, $99 value)
4. Each new customer is also offered the standard Pristine Circle intro (join membership → earn free detail)

**Why this works:**
- Social proof is local and immediate — "our neighbour Dan uses them" beats any ad
- The reward is proportional — three new customers is easily worth a $99 service
- Operationally: technicians are already travelling to a suburb; clustering bookings on the same street reduces drive time (bonus: briefly mention this to the customer — "if more of your building books in the same run, we can be more efficient with timing")
- Apartment buildings in South Yarra, Brighton, and Toorak are the priority — high density, high-income, multiple car owners per building

**Rollout: August**
- Identify the top 5 existing customers who live in apartment buildings or prestige streets
- Call or text personally (not mass email): "Hey [Name], we're trialling something for people in apartment buildings — if three neighbours join through you this month, your next detail is on us. Thought you'd be the right person to mention it to."
- Personal outreach converts far higher than a campaign

## Ambassador Tier: Activation Criteria for September

The July report proposed a "Pristine Ambassador" tier for customers who've referred 3+ members. With 5 weeks of the program running, identify anyone who has referred 2+ customers already — they are the pre-Ambassadors. Reaching out now, before the threshold, creates anticipation and accelerates the final referral.

**Ambassador Outreach Message (SMS, personalised):**

```
Hey [First Name] — you've already sent two mates to Pristine, which means a 
lot to us. One more referral this month and you're officially a Pristine 
Ambassador: priority booking, exclusive pricing, and your annual Revitalise 
Package on us ($300 value). Thought you'd want to know you're close. — Pristine
```

**What Ambassadors get (confirmed):**

| Benefit | Value | Frequency |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Annual Revitalise Package | $300 | 1× per year |
| Priority scheduling window | First access to new slots | Ongoing |
| Exclusive member pricing on add-ons | ~15% off | Ongoing |
| Named in private "Pristine Circle" community | Social recognition | Ongoing |

**Why the Revitalise is the right reward:**  
It's not a discount. It's an experience — decontamination, two-stage paint correction, deep clean, 6-month sealant. An Ambassador who comes in for a Revitalise gets a "wow" moment they'll talk about. The reward *is* the word of mouth.

## Updated Referral Messages: Spring Hook Edition

### Message 1 — Post-Service SMS (Spring Version)

**Send:** 24 hours after service, from now through October. Replace the standard SMS.

```
[First Name], your car's winter grime is gone — looks sharp. Spring's about 
3 weeks away. If you know someone who wants their car sorted before the 
warmer months, refer them to Pristine. You both get a free Maintenance Detail 
when they join a membership. Just reply with their name and number.
```

**Why the seasonal hook works:** It's not just a referral ask — it's timely. "Spring is 3 weeks away" creates urgency without pressure. The customer's car just got done; their friend's car hasn't. That contrast is the pitch.

**Changes from July version:**
- Seasonal hook (spring urgency) replaces the evergreen opening
- Shorter — the July version was comprehensive; this one is more direct
- Postscript targets pre-Ambassadors specifically, not all members
- CTA is "Who's the first person you thought of?" — this is a known copywriting technique that creates a mental image (a specific person) before the action

## The Loop at Month 2

```
WINTER DETAIL JOB
   → 24h SMS (spring hook): "Your car's sorted. Know someone?"
      → 1-to-1 referral (existing mechanic)
      → Community cluster: "Three neighbours = your next detail free"
         → Multiple new customers from one relationship
   → Pre-Ambassador outreach: "You're one away from Ambassador"
      → Final referral pushed over the line by anticipation
         → Ambassador earned → Revitalise reward → "wow" moment
            → Ambassador talks about it → organic mentions
               → New contacts reach out → loop restarts
```
