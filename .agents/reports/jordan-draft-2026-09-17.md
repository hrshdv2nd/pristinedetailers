## What Victoria's Tint Laws Actually Say

VicRoads enforces Visible Light Transmission (VLT) minimums — the percentage of light a window must allow through. Get this wrong and your car can fail a roadworthy.

| Window | Minimum VLT Required |
|---|---|
| Windscreen (below manufacturer AS2080 line) | 35% VLT |
| Front side windows (driver and passenger) | 35% VLT |
| Rear side windows | No restriction |
| Rear window | No restriction |

The practical upshot: front windows can be tinted, but not heavily. Most Melbourne drivers opt for 35% VLT on the front — it's the legal minimum and still noticeably cuts glare and cabin heat. On rear side windows and the back screen, you can go as dark as you want.

Worth knowing: a 35% VLT front tint still blocks around 65% of UV radiation. The tint is working even when it doesn't look dark from the outside.

If you're unsure about your existing tint's VLT, we check it with a tint meter before any new film goes on.

## How the Mobile Install Works

Our technician arrives with everything needed — film rolls, squeegees, heat gun, digital cutting tools, and a portable dust barrier for outdoor installs. Here's the process:

1. **Vehicle inspection** — we measure your glass and check for any existing film that needs removal before we quote final time
2. **Glass preparation** — every panel is cleaned with ammonia-free solution to strip contamination that causes bubbling post-install
3. **Film cutting** — panels are plotted digitally off your specific glass dimensions, not cut freehand on the car
4. **Application** — film applied wet, then squeegeed to eliminate moisture and air pockets
5. **Heat forming** — a heat gun shapes the film over curved rear glass so it sits flush with no lifting edges
6. **Final inspection** — we check every edge and corner before packing up

Expect minor haziness in the first 3–5 days as residual moisture cures out. Don't try to wipe it away — it clears on its own without any intervention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does window tinting take?**
Most vehicles take 2–4 hours. A large SUV with 7 windows runs closer to 4 hours. We don't cut cure time short.

**Can you tint in an underground car park?**
Yes. We work in underground car parks across Melbourne regularly. You need roughly 1.5 metres of clear working space around the vehicle. We bring portable lighting if the bay is dim.

**Does window tinting void my car warranty?**
No. Window tinting is an aftermarket modification and does not affect your statutory manufacturer warranty under Australian Consumer Law. If a dealer claims otherwise, ask them to point to it in the warranty document.

**What VLT is legal in Victoria for front windows?**
Front side windows must allow at least 35% of light through (35% VLT minimum) under VicRoads regulations. Rear side windows and the rear screen have no VLT restriction.

**Do I need to be present during the install?**
No. As long as we have access to the vehicle and a workable space, you don't need to be on-site. Most customers in apartment buildings leave a key with concierge or a neighbour.
