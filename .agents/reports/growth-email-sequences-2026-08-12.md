## This Week's Focus

The four standard lifecycle emails (booking confirmation, post-service, 30-day win-back, membership nudge) were built in the June 10 report. They stand — no rework needed.

This week: **Post-Ceramic Coating & PPF Warranty Sequence.**

This is a gap. Our ceramic coating and PPF customers represent the highest-ticket transactions in the business ($750–$3,000+). They're the most trust-forward customers — they handed us a Porsche, a Range Rover, a GT3. And they have warranty obligations that require professional maintenance at specific intervals. That's a built-in reason to be in their inbox with genuinely useful information across multiple years.

We're not using it.

---

## The Case for This Sequence

**Why ceramic/PPF buyers are different:**

1. **Higher LTV potential** — someone who's already spent $2,500 on PPF is not price-sensitive. They're value-sensitive. A $149/month Signature membership is an easy conversation.

2. **Warranty creates urgency** — the 8-year manufacturer warranty on ceramic is only valid if the customer maintains the coating professionally. This is a real obligation, not a marketing construct. Which means we have a legitimate, high-value reason to contact them at 8 weeks, 6 months, 12 months, and before warranty expiry.

3. **Social proof potential** — a ceramic coating customer 12 months in, whose car still looks factory-new, is the most credible referrer we have. They've seen the long game.

4. **Natural membership conversion point** — the maintenance obligation is exactly what membership is designed to solve. The pitch writes itself.

## Email 1 — Post-Service: Warranty Confirmation & Curing Guide

```
Trigger:     Service marked complete — ceramic or PPF job
Send timing: Immediately
Goal:        Set expectations for the curing window, deliver warranty documentation, 
             establish Pristine as the authority on their coating
Exit:        N/A (transactional — all customers receive this)
```

**Subject:** Your ceramic coating is curing. Here's what the next 7 days mean.  
**Preview:** The bond forms in the first week. What you do now determines how it performs for years.

---

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

[Tech name] has finished the [ceramic coating / PPF installation] on your [Car Make/Model].

The result is where it should be — but the next 7 days matter. Here's what you need to know.

---

**[CERAMIC COATING BLOCK]**

The coating is bonding chemically with your clear coat right now. This curing process takes 5–7 days to complete. During that window:

- **No washing** — no rinse, no automatic wash, no hand wash. Let it cure.
- **Covered parking where possible** — not mandatory, but ideal for the first week
- **No waxes, polishes, or spray detailers** — these will interfere with the bond
- **Bird droppings: wipe immediately** — the coating will repel them fully after curing, but in the first week, act fast. A damp microfibre is enough.

After day 7, the chemistry is set. You'll see the water sheeting properly — that's the hydrophobic layer working. It won't wear off like wax. It's bonded to the clear coat.

---

**[PPF BLOCK]**

The film is installed. For the first 72 hours:

- **No high-pressure washing** — let the adhesive set fully
- **No brush-contact car washes** — edges can lift on fresh installs
- **Small moisture pockets near edges are normal** — they'll disappear within 1–3 weeks as the film adheres and dries

After the first week, the adhesive is fully set. The self-healing layer is active. Light surface scratches will disappear in warm conditions or with a pour of warm water.

---

**Your warranty:**

Your coating carries a **[X]-year manufacturer warranty**. To keep it valid:

- Professional maintenance service every 6 months
- Approved products only — no generic waxes, no acidic wheel cleaners on coated surfaces

We'll contact you when your first maintenance window is due. Or — if you'd rather not track intervals — our **Signature membership** covers bi-monthly details with ceramic maintenance included. Members' coatings never fall out of spec.

## Growth Loop Summary

Every ceramic or PPF job is the beginning of a multi-year relationship, not a transaction. The sequence above is designed to make that true in practice — not just in principle.

The loop:

```
Premium service (£750–$3,000) 
→ Warranty confirmation (trust + legitimacy)  
→ 8-week maintenance (first repeat booking — easiest sale we'll ever make)  
→ 6-month service ("you'd already have had three of these") → Membership  
→ 12-month anniversary ("mention your name") → Referral  
→ Pre-expiry → Renewal or PPF upgrade  
→ Loop resets
```

A ceramic customer who converts to Signature membership, maintains annually, and refers one person per year is worth approximately:

- $149/mo × 12 = $1,788/year in recurring membership revenue
- 1 referral/year → average first booking ~$250–$500 → potential new member
- Renewal at year 3 or year 8 → another $750–$2,500 job

That's a 3-year LTV in the range of $5,000–$7,500 from a customer who started with a single ceramic coating booking.

This sequence is how we capture that value instead of leaving it in the "we did a good job and never heard from them again" bucket.
