**Caption:**

One of the most common questions we get from Melbourne car owners: "Should I get PPF or ceramic coating?"

They're not competing options — they do completely different jobs. PPF (paint protection film) is a physical barrier: a self-healing polyurethane layer over your most vulnerable panels that stops stone chips and road debris before they reach your paint. Ceramic coating is a chemical bond with your clear coat: it repels UV, bird droppings, water spots, and contamination for up to 8 years without lifting or peeling.

The approach we recommend for any prestige vehicle in Melbourne — especially if you're doing regular freeway kilometres on the Eastern or Monash — is the stack: PPF on the high-impact zones (bonnet leading edge, full bumper, mirrors, A-pillars), ceramic coating over everything. You get physical protection where it matters most and chemical protection across the whole car.

## Platform: LinkedIn

**Caption:**

Most professionals who ask about paint protection for their vehicle are treating it as a binary decision: PPF or ceramic coating. It isn't one.

Paint protection film and ceramic coating are layered solutions that solve different physics problems. PPF is a mechanical barrier — a self-healing polyurethane film bonded to high-impact surfaces (bonnet, front bumper, mirrors, A-pillars, door edges) that absorbs the kinetic energy of stone chips and road debris before they can fracture the clear coat. Ceramic coating is a chemical bond — a nano-ceramic layer cured onto the clear coat that creates a hydrophobic, UV-stable surface resistant to bird acid, industrial fallout, and oxidation for up to 8 years with a manufacturer warranty.

Applied correctly, they work in sequence: PPF on vulnerable panels first, ceramic coating over the entire vehicle on top. The result is a car that is physically protected where it faces the highest impact risk and chemically protected across the whole surface. For a vehicle worth $80K–$300K, this isn't a cosmetic decision — it's an asset management one. Resale values on properly protected prestige vehicles are measurably stronger; paint in original condition commands a premium at trade-in that typically exceeds the cost of protection applied at purchase.
