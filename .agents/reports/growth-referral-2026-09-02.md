## Spring Activation: September Is the Best Time to Push Referrals

### Why September Works

Melbourne's Spring creates a natural trigger: car owners emerge from winter wanting their car to look right again. Bird activity peaks in Toorak and Brighton (nesting season — droppings are aggressive). The social calendar opens up: Sunday drives, car shows, Cars & Coffee events restart.

**The emotional state:** "My car's been sitting through winter — it needs attention. And honestly, so do my mates' cars."

This is the highest-willingness-to-refer moment of the year. A customer whose car just got a Spring Revitalise is not just satisfied — they're proud. That's the referral trigger.

### Core Mechanic (unchanged — still sound)

> **"Refer a mate. You both get a free Maintenance Detail."**
>
> When a friend joins any Pristine membership (Essential or Signature), both parties receive one complimentary Maintenance Detail ($99 value).

### Spring Overlay (time-limited urgency, September–November only)

Add a seasonal sweetener for the Spring push:

> **"Refer before 30 November and get your Spring Detail bumped to a Full Maintenance Detail — we'll add a clay bar and iron decon at no charge."**

**What this does:**
- Adds $30–40 in service value, costs little in labour time
- Creates genuine urgency without being gimmicky
- "Clay bar and iron decon" resonates with the ICP — they know what this means
- Naturally clusters referral activity into Q4 before the summer slowdown in January

## Pristine Ambassador Tier: Activation Criteria

The July report flagged the Ambassador tier for activation at 100+ members. If we're approaching or past that milestone, it's time to formalise.

### "Pristine Ambassador" Program

**Who qualifies:** Any member who has referred 3 or more friends who joined a membership

**What they get:**
1. **Annual complimentary Revitalise Package** ($300 value) — booked at a time of their choosing, priority scheduled
2. **"First Access" booking window** — a private 48h window before new booking slots open to the public (relevant when demand peaks in Spring/Summer)
3. **Named in the Pristine community** — their name (or first name + suburb) added to a "Pristine Circle" page or card: *"Marcus T., Hawthorn — Porsche GT3 owner and Pristine member since 2024"*
4. **One-time upgrade offer** — if on Essential, a 3-month trial of Signature at Essential pricing (a soft upgrade nudge that converts ~25% of trialists)

**Launch the Ambassador tier with an email to anyone who has referred 1–2 people already** — they're one or two referrals from Ambassador status. Tell them they're close:

> *"You've already referred [1/2] friend(s) to Pristine. Refer one more who joins a membership and you unlock Pristine Ambassador status — including an annual Revitalise Package (worth $300) on us. You're almost there."*

This "almost there" progress frame converts at significantly higher rates than a cold offer.

## New Mechanic: Cars & Coffee Referral Moment

Melbourne's Cars & Coffee events (St Kilda, Brighton, Toorak) restart in Spring. Our ICP — the Porsche GT3 owner, the Ferrari weekend driver, the Tesla Plaid commuter — attends these.

**The mechanic:** Equip technicians and any team members attending with a physical "refer a mate" card:

> **Front:** [Car photo] "Your car looked like this once. It can again."  
> **Back:** "Ask [Technician Name] about the Pristine membership. Mention this card — you'll both get a free Maintenance Detail."

**How it works at Cars & Coffee:**
- Technician (or an Ambassador member) hands the card to someone admiring a Pristine-detailed car in the car park
- The recipient books, mentions the card / referrer name
- Both get the reward
- No codes, no tracking software needed — just a name at the time of booking

**Why this works:** The referral happens *in context* — at a moment of genuine car enthusiasm, from someone whose car is visibly a proof point. The social proof is standing 15 feet away.

**Cost to launch:** Business card printing (~$50 for 250 cards). Zero other setup required.

## The Loop Reminder

A great detailing job → an SMS ask 24 hours later → a referred friend → a membership join → a free detail that creates another great job → another SMS ask. Each loop takes 4–6 weeks. By the time the jacarandas have dropped their flowers, Pristine should have 5–10 referral loops running simultaneously across Melbourne's Inner East and Bayside suburbs.

The spring window is the best organic growth moment of the year. The program is designed. The messages are ready. The only variable is whether we send them.
