## Platform: LinkedIn

**Caption:**

Before any coating goes on a car at our studio, we spend more time on prep than on the coating itself.

That's not inefficiency — that's the job.

A ceramic or graphene coating bonds chemically to your clear coat. Whatever's underneath — swirl marks from years of automated washes, bird dropping etch, road contamination — gets locked in permanently. You won't see it until it's too late, and correcting it after coating means removing the coating first. The prep stage is where the result is decided, not the application.

So we decontaminate, clay bar, polish, and inspect before a single drop of product touches the paint. For an owner who's spent $80K–$200K on a vehicle, that's the standard they're paying for. It's also why our coatings carry manufacturer warranties up to 9 years — the certification requires the prep, not just the product.

If you're considering coating protection for a company car or a vehicle you plan to hold and resell, the prep question is worth asking before you book anywhere. Happy to walk through what's involved for your specific car — DM us or enquire via our website.
