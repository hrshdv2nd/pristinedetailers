## Platform: LinkedIn

**Caption:**

Most car protection products on the market share the same problem: they wear off.

Spray waxes, polymer sealants, even many consumer-grade ceramic sprays all work by sitting on top of your clear coat. They're effective for weeks, sometimes months — but they wash away, oxidise, or mechanically abrade. You're applying them repeatedly and your paint is only as protected as your last application.

Professional ceramic coating works through chemical adhesion, not topical layering. The nano-ceramic compound bonds to the clear coat's molecular structure, creating a surface that becomes part of the paint itself. The result is a hydrophobic, UV-stable, contaminant-resistant barrier — with manufacturer-backed warranties of up to 8 years on ceramic and 9 years on graphene from Ceramic Pro and Gtechniq. One installation, warrantied for the duration of most ownership and lease cycles.
