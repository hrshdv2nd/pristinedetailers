The prep run correctly identified three buyer types: the winter-damage auditor, the pre-coating researcher, and the spring-reset buyer. Now that it's actually September — not a calendar projection — the timing sharpens.

Swirl marks visible in spring sun that were hidden under overcast winter skies are the exact moment-of-intent this campaign is built for. The "spring sunlight finds every swirl mark" angle is no longer a prediction — it's current experience. This Wednesday set leans into that immediacy.

**AFL Grand Final timing:** The 2026 Grand Final is scheduled for 26 September — three and a half weeks away. Buyers attending in prestige cars (Toorak clients driving to the Birdcage precinct, Bayside buyers doing Saturday drives to Sorrento for the spring racing lead-up) are now actively thinking about their car's appearance. This is the highest-attention window of the year for appearance-focused Melbourne car owners.

## Responsive Search Ad — Paint Correction Melbourne (Wednesday Variant)

### Headlines (30 chars max)

| # | Headline | Chars | Angle |
|---|----------|-------|-------|
| H1 | Paint Correction Melbourne | 26 | Keyword / intent anchor |
| H2 | Spring Light Finds Every Flaw | 29 | Seasonal trigger — immediate |
| H3 | Swirl Marks Fixed. One Visit. | 29 | Outcome + delivery model |
| H4 | From $300. Certified Techs. | 27 | Price anchor + credibility |
| H5 | Book Paint Correction Today | 27 | Direct CTA |

**Pin guidance:**
- Pin H1 to Position 1 — keyword match confirms intent to the buyer who already knows the service name; no pitch, just confirmation this ad is theirs.
- H2 and H3 rotate in Position 2 — H2 ("Spring Light Finds Every Flaw") targets the buyer who has already noticed the problem but hasn't yet named it as "paint correction"; it mirrors the moment of recognition without naming the symptom, which reduces cognitive load. H3 ("Swirl Marks Fixed. One Visit.") speaks to the buyer who has researched enough to know the service and wants confirmation it's fast and complete — "One Visit" eliminates the workshop-multiple-drop-off objection in two words.
- H4 and H5 rotate in Position 3 — H4 anchors price and signals that a certified technician (not a casual weekend detailer) is doing the work; the full stop after $300 paces the read so "Certified Techs" lands as a separate, confirmatory claim. H5 is a direct action headline that functions as a nudge for the buyer already in decision mode.

**Why "Spring Light Finds Every Flaw" works as the seasonal hook:** The buyer in this moment didn't search "paint correction" because they read an article — they searched because they looked at their car in September light and saw something they haven't seen since autumn. The headline mirrors that experience without requiring them to have any knowledge of the service. It earns the click by demonstrating that the ad understands what just happened to them.

| D2 | Required before ceramic coating. 4.9 stars, 2,400+ cars. Toorak to Bayside. Book now. | 85 |

"Machine polish" is the technical term buyers use once they've moved past the awareness stage — it self-selects for the right intent. "Mobile — we come to you" closes the workshop objection. The em dash slows the copy at the right moment.

**D2 rationale:** "Required before ceramic coating" captures the pre-coating buyer mid-journey — a buyer researching ceramic will land on this and realise paint correction is the first step, which extends their intent to a $300+ booking now and a $750+ coating booking within 30 days. The social proof stack (4.9 stars, 2,400+ cars) is placed after the framing to substantiate rather than lead with a claim. "Toorak to Bayside" names the postcodes the ICP self-identifies with — it reads as local authority, not a service-area disclaimer.

## Keyword Recommendations

### Exact Match — 5 Keywords

```
[paint correction Melbourne]
[swirl mark removal Melbourne]
[car scratch removal Melbourne]
[machine polish Melbourne]
[paint correction before ceramic coating]
```

**Rationale (unchanged from 08-30 prep; confirmed appropriate for live campaign):**
- `[paint correction Melbourne]` — primary intent term; anchor keyword; must be owned with exact match to prevent spend bleed into kits, courses, and jobs listings
- `[swirl mark removal Melbourne]` — symptom-based search; buyer has identified the problem before they know the service name; this term captures moment-of-recognition intent
- `[car scratch removal Melbourne]` — wider damage framing; captures light scratch intent at top of funnel; ad copy should qualify (machine polish addresses light scratches and swirl marks, not panel damage or deep chips)
- `[machine polish Melbourne]` — technical buyer who has done research and is comparing providers on skill; "Certified Techs" in H4 speaks directly to this searcher's evaluation criteria
- `[paint correction before ceramic coating]` — highest-intent long-tail; buyer is in active research on ceramic and has reached the prerequisite question; lowest volume, highest conversion likelihood; route to landing page variant with ceramic upsell visible above the fold

---

### Negative Keywords — 3 to Exclude

```
[DIY paint correction]
[paint correction course]
[panel beater Melbourne]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[DIY paint correction]` — explicit self-service intent; will click, bounce, and degrade Quality Score for target terms
- `[paint correction course]` — buyer wants to learn the skill, not hire; anti-persona for a premium mobile service
- `[panel beater Melbourne]` — structural bodywork intent; incompatible with cosmetic paint correction; attracts unqualified traffic that bounces on the service description

## Mid-Campaign Optimisation Notes (Wednesday Check-In)

The campaign launched Monday 1 September. Early signals to watch by end of week:

**If CTR is below 4%:** The seasonal angle (H2 — "Spring Light Finds Every Flaw") may be too conceptual for intent-based search. Swap H2 for a direct benefit variant: "Swirl Marks Gone. We Come to You" (32 chars — trim to) "Swirl Marks Gone. Mobile." (25 chars). Intent-based search rewards specificity over creative angles more than display or social.

**If Ad Group 3 is converting:** The pre-coating buyer segment is working — consider adding a third keyword `[paint correction cost Melbourne]` to the ad group to capture buyers in price-research mode. Route to a landing page with transparent pricing ($300 Revitalise package) and ceramic upsell ($750+) visible.

**If Ad Group 2 is showing high bounce rate:** The light-scratch buyer landing on the paint correction page may be seeing a mismatch (they wanted scratch repair, not paint correction). Add a qualifying line to D1 for Ad Group 2: swap "Machine polish removes swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation." for "Machine polish removes light scratches, swirl marks, and oxidation." — the word "light" pre-qualifies so panel-beater intent self-excludes before clicking.

**Budget timing:** Grand Final is 26 September. Allocate 20–30% extra daily budget to the week of 21–26 September — appearance-focused Melbourne buyers peak in that window. The week of the Grand Final should have the highest daily caps of the entire Paint Correction campaign.
