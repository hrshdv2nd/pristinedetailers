# Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads — Ceramic Coating
**Date:** 2026-08-12 (Week 33)
**Service Focus:** Ceramic Coating (from $750, up to 8-year manufacturer warranty)
**Platforms:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories/Reels (adapt format)

---

## Service Context

Ceramic coating = nano-ceramic bond on the clear coat. Hydrophobic, UV-stable, scratch-resistant. Pristine's point of difference: certified technicians come to the customer's home, office, or car park. No workshop drop-off. From $750 — positioned as protection for a $50K–$200K+ asset.

**Key customer fear:** "I've spent a lot on this car and I don't want to ruin the paint."
**Primary objection on Meta:** "How can mobile be as good as a fixed studio?"

*Target: New car owners, 30–50yo, $100K+ cars, Inner East Melbourne*

**Primary Text (104 chars — feed visible):**
> Your $80K car is losing value every time it sits unprotected. Ceramic coating from $750. We come to you.

**Headline (24 chars):**
> Protect Your Paint Value

**Description (24 chars):**
> Mobile. Certified. $750+

**Visual brief:**
- Split-frame or before/after: Left — dull, watermarked paint with a bird dropping or swirl mark visible. Right — deep gloss finish with water beading in sheets.
- No faces. The car is the hero.
- Caption overlay (lower third): "From $750. We come to you."
- Car type: Range Rover Sport or Tesla Model S — recognisable to the Toorak/Brighton audience

**Tone:** Matter-of-fact. Not "amazing results!" — just the physics of what an unprotected paint does over time, and the fix.

**Interest targeting (stack these — AND logic where possible):**

- Behaviours: Purchased vehicle in last 12 months (if available), Frequent travellers (income proxy)
- Affluent homeowners in target postcodes

**Demographics:** Age 28–55, all genders (skew budget 65/35 male/female based on historical car enthusiast data — review after 14 days)

**Exclusions:**
- Existing customers (upload customer list as exclusion)
- People who already converted on the booking page

### Secondary Audience — Lookalike

- 1–2% lookalike of existing Pristine customer email list in VIC
- If LTV data exists, base lookalike on top 20% customers (not all customers)
- Test: 1% (tightest match) vs. 2% (broader reach) in separate ad sets — don't stack them

### Campaign Structure

```
Campaign: META_Conv_CeramicCoating_Melbourne_Aug26
├── Ad Set 1: Cold — Postcode + Interests
│   ├── Variant 1: Investment Protection (static image)
│   ├── Variant 2: Mobile Convenience (static image)
│   └── Variant 3: Social Proof (video or static)
├── Ad Set 2: Lookalike 1% VIC
│   └── Best-performing variant from Ad Set 1 (after 7 days)
└── Ad Set 3: Retargeting — Website Visitors (see below)
```

**Budget split:** 60% cold postcode/interest, 25% lookalike, 15% retargeting.
**Minimum budget:** $25/day per active ad set to exit learning phase within 7 days.

**Visual brief:** Clean, minimal. The car (a BMW or Range Rover silhouette against a white background, or a water-bead close-up) — no busy layout. This person already knows who Pristine is; don't re-explain. Create urgency through simplicity.

## Creative Notes for Production

1. **Water bead footage is your strongest visual asset.** A 6–10 second loop of ceramic-coated paint shedding water outperforms any graphic for this category. If you don't have this, shoot it in the next 2 sessions.

2. **Don't use stock car photography.** Melbourne premium car owners will clock it immediately. Use real cars from real customer jobs — get permission if faces appear.

3. **Instagram Stories sizing (9:16):** Variant 2 (mobile/convenience) works best here — the "we come to you" message fits the platform's domestic/lifestyle feel. Resize the Toorak garage visual to vertical.

4. **Caption all video.** 85% of feed video is watched without sound. Key message must read in the first 3 seconds as text on screen.

5. **Variant 3 (testimonial) should be tested as a dark post first** — push it to the Lookalike audience before the broad postcode audience to validate proof credibility before spending cold budget on it.
