Mobile car detailing in Melbourne works exactly the way it sounds: a certified technician drives to your home, office, or apartment car park and performs a full detail on-site. You need a 240V power point and an outdoor tap within 15 metres. We operate across 60+ Melbourne suburbs — your home, office, or apartment car park.

There's no drop-off, no waiting room, no arranging a lift back from an industrial suburb. You book a time, and the technician arrives with everything — pressure washer, polishing machine, chemical inventory, generator backup. The only thing they use from your location is water and electricity.

## What happens during a mobile detail?

The exact process depends on the service level. A maintenance detail — wash, decontamination iron fallout removal, and a sealant wipe-down — typically runs 2–3 hours. A full Revitalise Package, which includes two-stage paint correction, deep interior clean, and a 6-month sealant, takes 5–6 hours.

For ceramic coating, plan for a full day: the car needs to be clean, decontaminated, and paint-corrected before any coating goes on. That's a non-negotiable sequence — applying ceramic to unpolished paint just seals in the scratches.

PPF installation follows the same logic. Partial-front coverage takes 4–6 hours. Full-vehicle wrap takes two days.

## Can mobile detailers come to Melbourne apartments?

Yes — and we do, regularly.

Apartment detailing is common across inner Melbourne. The key is access to a power point (often available via the car park's charging bays or building utility outlets) and a tap (typically a hose bib in the basement or near the bin area). Building management approval is sometimes required for wet work — it's worth checking with your building manager before booking.

Suburbs we regularly service within apartment car parks include **South Yarra**, **Richmond**, **Prahran**, **Hawthorn**, and **Toorak**. Each has its own building quirks — underground bays in South Yarra's apartment towers tend to be tight, Richmond warehouse conversions often have better water access than you'd expect, and strata rules in Toorak highrise buildings vary considerably.

If you're in a building that restricts water use in the basement, we can still perform dry services: interior detailing, ceramic coating top-ups, and PPF installation don't require a water source.

## How to book

Bookings go through the Pristine Detailers website. Choose your service, pick a time slot, and enter your address. If your location is an apartment or commercial car park, add the access notes in the comments field — entry codes, parking level, nearest tap location.

We'll confirm the booking and send a pre-visit checklist. On the day, the technician arrives in a fully equipped van. You don't need to be present for the full duration, but we ask that someone is available for the first 10 minutes to confirm access and hand over any entry details.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you come to my underground car park?**
Yes. We work in underground car parks across Melbourne regularly. The requirements are a 240V outlet and tap access within 15 metres. Some building management teams ask for prior notice — we can provide a service description letter if needed.

**What if I don't have outdoor water access?**
Call us before booking. In most cases we can adapt — interior services and some coating work don't require water. For wash-based services, we'll need to confirm a water source exists at your location.

**Do I need to be home during the detail?**
Not for the full duration. We ask that someone is available at the start to confirm access and hand over any entry codes. After that, you're free to leave — we'll contact you when the job is complete and send through photos of the finished car.

**Can you park in our strata car park?**
Yes, with building approval. We can provide a written description of the work and the equipment we bring if your strata committee requires it. Most strata managers approve it without issue.
