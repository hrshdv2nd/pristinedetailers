## Platform: Facebook

**Caption:**

If your car has been through a drive-through wash at any point in the last year, it probably has swirl marks — and most owners have no idea, because they're only visible when the sun hits right.

Swirl marks are microscopic scratches left by brush contact and dry chamois wipes. On darker cars especially, they accumulate into a dull grey haze that kills the depth of the paint. The only fix is paint correction: a machine polishing process that removes a controlled amount of clear coat to level the surface — not fill it, actually remove the damage.

We do the full correction in a single mobile visit, to your home, driveway, or office car park. If you're also planning to protect the car with ceramic coating afterward, correction is always the first step — you don't seal swirls under a coating that's built to last 5–8 years.

## Platform: LinkedIn

**Caption:**

The paint correction conversation is one most car owners have too late.

By the time swirl marks are obvious — the grey haze on a black bonnet in direct sunlight, the flattened reflections on a dark metallic — the clear coat has been compromised by dozens of drive-through wash passes and dry chamois wipes. The damage is cumulative and invisible under overcast skies, which means most owners in Melbourne's grey winter don't realise how bad it actually is until the sun comes out in spring.

Two-stage paint correction — a cutting phase to remove damaged clear coat, a refining phase to restore reflective depth — brings the finish back to factory clarity. For a prestige vehicle, the difference before and after is significant enough that owners routinely describe it as "better than when I bought it." From a valuation standpoint, corrected paint at trade-in is worth more: a buyer doesn't deduct corrective work costs from their offer when there's nothing to correct.

If you're planning to invest in ceramic coating, paint correction is the non-negotiable prerequisite. A nano-ceramic bond applied over swirled clear coat seals the damage in for the next 5–8 years — and you've spent $750+ to protect a compromised surface. The correction stage ensures the coating bonds clean and performs for its full rated life, with the 8-year manufacturer warranty actually meaning something. The combination of correction plus ceramic is what gives a car its long-term finish — not the coating alone.

We carry out paint correction across Melbourne at your home, office, or secure car park. If your vehicle has been through any automated or budget wash service in the past year, a paint assessment is worth having before any coating conversation. DM us or enquire via our website to arrange one.
