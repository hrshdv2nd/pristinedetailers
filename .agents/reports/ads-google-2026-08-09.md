## Seasonal Context

Mid-August is the highest-intent window of the year for paint correction, for two compounding reasons:

1. **Five months of Melbourne winter damage.** Since March, cars in Toorak, Hawthorn, and Bayside have been through cold-weather washes with dirty chamois, contaminated rinse water, and grime-heavy roads. Bird droppings from inner-city plane trees and Norfolk Island pines sit longer in winter — owners wash less often, and acidic contamination etches into clear coat while the car sits in an underground car park for days. By August, swirl marks and micro-scratches are visible on any car with a dark paint colour under direct light. Owners are noticing.

2. **Pre-spring setup intent.** Smart Melbourne car owners know that paint correction is the mandatory first step before any ceramic coating or PPF job. September and October are the busiest months for ceramic applications (buyers want protection locked in before summer heat and UV). Buyers searching for paint correction in August are often planning a ceramic coating and understand — or have just learned — that the correction has to happen first. This is a buyer who has already decided to spend money on paint care; the question is only where they book.

These two audiences — the reactive buyer (swirls are already visible) and the proactive pre-coating buyer — need slightly different ad angles. The ad groups below split them.

- `[machine polish Melbourne]` — process-aware buyer who knows what tool delivers the result; this signals a more-researched buyer who is evaluating whether the technician is using a professional machine (not a hand polish), which is exactly the buyer Pristine's certified technicians are positioned to convert
- `[mobile paint correction Melbourne]` — buyer has specifically decided they want a mobile operator; this filters out everyone willing to drop off at a fixed studio, leaving only the buyers for whom the come-to-you model is a requirement, not a preference; Pristine's primary differentiator converts hardest against this query
- `[paint correction Toorak]` — suburb-specific intent signals a buyer who is location-confirmed and ready to book; Toorak is Pristine's highest-density postcode for Porsche, Tesla, and European ownership; a buyer typing the suburb has eliminated the "are they near me?" question and moved to "who do I book?"

### Negative Keywords — 3 to Exclude

```
[paint correction compound]
[touch up paint]
[how to remove swirl marks]
```

**Rationale:**
- `[paint correction compound]` — buyer is purchasing a product (Menzerna, Meguiar's M105, etc.) to apply themselves; zero service intent; will click, read that Pristine is a professional service, and bounce immediately; excluding this term prevents spend on the largest DIY buyer cohort in the category
- `[touch up paint]` — buyer is looking for a touch-up pen or aerosol for a chip or scratch; this is a fundamentally different problem (paint fill vs. paint refinement) and a fundamentally different price sensitivity (they want a $15 pen, not a $300 correction); self-filtering intent with no conversion pathway
- `[how to remove swirl marks]` — informational query from a DIY buyer in the research phase; this buyer may eventually become a service customer but requires extensive nurture; the cost-per-click for this term is wasted on buyers who will not convert on first click, and the landing page is not structured to capture research-phase intent; exclude from paid and retarget organically via SEO/content instead

## Context Notes

- **Seasonal timing:** August is peak swirl-mark awareness season (winter washes, bird droppings, reduced wash frequency) and the highest-intent window for pre-spring ceramic coating prep. Both audiences are active now. The spend-to-conversion ratio is typically strongest in the fortnight before spring detailing season kicks off — launch Monday 10 August to capture this window before competitors increase bids in September.
- **Melbourne-specific hooks used:** Toorak, Brighton, Mornington Peninsula, South Yarra in keywords and descriptions; swirl marks from winter roads are the named pain point; two-stage machine correction differentiates from franchise mobile washes (which operate across both Bayside and Inner East)
- **Tone check:** No superlatives ("best paint correction in Melbourne" is excluded per brand voice); "two-stage polish" not "cutting-edge technology"; "machine polish removes swirls" not "we offer comprehensive paint restoration services"; "no workshop" not "convenient mobile option"
- **Paint correction vs. ceramic:** This ad set does not mention ceramic pricing or booking. The correction buyer is at a different decision stage — they need the confidence that Pristine can fix their current defects before they consider the protection upgrade. The ceramic upsell is triggered post-conversion, not in the acquisition ad. Mixing correction and ceramic copy in one ad dilutes intent, confuses the buyer about what they are clicking, and reduces quality score on both terms.
- **Pre-launch creative check:** Before activating, confirm there is a before/after visual asset on the `/services/paint-correction` landing page. The paint correction purchase decision is more visual than PPF or ceramic — buyers need to see swirl-mark removal in action, not read about it. If the landing page is copy-heavy without a comparison image, the ad will convert visitors at a lower rate than the copy quality deserves.
