## Seasonal Context

Week 48 falls inside the highest-value paint correction booking window of the Melbourne calendar year. Three signals converge right now:

1. **Pre-summer finish urgency** — Melbourne summer starts in earnest in December. Car owners in Toorak, Brighton, and Bayside who've been putting off paint correction for months are now forced to confront the reality: their car has accumulated six months of swirl marks from car washes, bird dropping etch from Toorak's plane trees, and car park scratches from CBD decks. If they want their paint looking correct before Christmas parties and Peninsula drives, they have a narrow window to book. This is the highest-intent moment in the paint correction calendar. The copy must reflect that the urgency is real and Melbourne-specific — not invented.

2. **Peninsula pre-season prep** — The Mornington Peninsula summer rental season begins in December. Owners who store cars at Portsea or Sorrento beach houses, or who make weekly Peninsula runs from December through February, treat November as the correction and protection window. A buyer in this segment searches "paint correction Melbourne" in late November because they want the correction done before coating, and they want both done before the summer starts. The ad must connect to this sequencing reality: correction now, coating on top, then onto the Peninsula.

3. **Post-Melbourne Cup, pre-Christmas prestige window** — Melbourne Cup is behind them. The next social car event is Christmas and New Year's. For the ICP in South Yarra or Hawthorn who treats their car as a social statement — Porsche, Tesla, Range Rover — having their paint corrected before the summer social calendar opens is not vanity; it is maintenance of the investment. The search intent in this window is purchase-ready, not research-ready. Copy must confirm the studio's credentials and the booking path, not explain what paint correction is.

All three segments are search-active in late November. A single RSA with five headlines covers the keyword match, the problem trigger, the certification signal, the seasonal urgency, and the Peninsula pre-season intent. Google's machine learning will weight the combinations that convert most efficiently per segment across the campaign run.

**Headline notes:**

**H1 — "Paint Correction Melbourne"** is the keyword headline. It aligns exactly with the highest-volume search term for this service category in Melbourne ([paint correction Melbourne]) and confirms geographic relevance without consuming headline space with brand language. Pin to Position 1 in Ad Group 2 to ensure keyword match for every impression on the primary term. This headline does not sell — it confirms the buyer is in the right place, which is the job of a keyword headline.

**H2 — "Swirls & Scratches Removed"** is the problem/solution headline. It names the two most common complaints a paint correction buyer searches for. Swirl marks (from automatic car washes and incorrect hand washing) and car park scratches (from tight CBD decks and valet parking) are the dominant damage types for the Toorak/Bayside ICP. The headline works because it names the problem, not the category. A buyer searching "swirl mark removal Melbourne" reads this as direct confirmation they've found the right service. Pin to Position 1 in Ad Group 1 (problem-aware searches).

**H3 — "Gtechniq-Certified Studio"** is the credentials headline. For a buyer with a prestige car who has been burned by an uncertified installer or a generic detailer, certification is the trust signal that separates Pristine from competitors. "Studio" distinguishes the facility from a mobile detailer. This headline does not close — it qualifies. It tells the buyer that this is a certified operation before they click. Use in combination with H1 or H4 to pair the keyword with a credibility signal.

**H4 — "Book Before December Fills"** is the scheduling urgency headline. It reflects the actual studio capacity reality in Melbourne's prestige car correction calendar: November and December bookings fill from both paint correction and pre-coating prep work (buyers who want correction done before a ceramic or graphene coating in January). The month anchor ("December") makes the urgency factual rather than manufactured. For a buyer who has been procrastinating on correction for months, this headline creates the trigger to act today rather than "next week."

**H5 — "Prep Your Car for Summer"** is the seasonal intent headline. It speaks to the buyer who is not yet searching "paint correction Melbourne" with specific problem language — they are searching with seasonal framing ("pre-summer car care", "car prep for summer Melbourne"). The headline connects correction to the summer prep mindset of the Toorak/Bayside/Peninsula ICP. It also signals to the buyer that this is a timely, seasonal action rather than a year-round service they can defer indefinitely. This headline works best in the second half of November and through mid-December.

**Keyword notes:**

[paint correction Melbourne] is the primary volume term. This is the category search — the buyer has already decided they need correction and is searching for who does it in Melbourne. Bid allocation should be heaviest on this term. It captures all three seasonal segments (swirl removal, pre-summer prep, Peninsula pre-season).

[swirl mark removal Melbourne] and [car scratch removal Melbourne] are the problem-aware terms. A buyer searching either of these terms has identified a specific damage type and is looking for a solution. These buyers are often earlier in the journey than a [paint correction Melbourne] searcher — they know they have a problem but may not know the professional service category name. H2 ("Swirls & Scratches Removed") maps directly to these searches and should be pinned to Position 1 for this ad group.

[paint correction Toorak] is the postcode-qualifier term. A buyer adding a suburb name to their correction search is a higher-intent, higher-ICP signal than a generic Melbourne search. The postcode specificity suggests they want a studio near them and are comparing local options. This term is lower volume but higher conversion probability; use a bid modifier of +20–30% above the base Melbourne terms.

[machine polish Melbourne] is the trade-aware term. A buyer who searches "machine polish" (rather than "paint correction" or "swirl removal") has either researched the correction process or is a car enthusiast who knows the method. This buyer is a strong candidate for the Car Enthusiast persona — they want to know the studio uses the right equipment and technique, not just that they "remove scratches." H3 ("Gtechniq-Certified Studio") and D1 (certified techs) will resonate most strongly with this search.

## Ad Group Structure Recommendation

Run **two ad groups** within the same Paint Correction campaign:

**Ad Group 1 — Problem-Aware (Swirl & Scratch Removal)**
- Keywords: [swirl mark removal Melbourne], [car scratch removal Melbourne]
- Pin H2 ("Swirls & Scratches Removed") to Position 1
- Pin D1 (certified techs, bird etch, Melbourne location signal) to Description Position 1
- Audience overlay: Remarketing — visitors to /services and /paint-correction pages in the last 30 days (bid modifier +30%)
- Bidding: Maximise Clicks until 20 conversions, then switch to Target CPA

**Ad Group 2 — In-Market (Paint Correction Intent)**
- Keywords: [paint correction Melbourne], [paint correction Toorak], [machine polish Melbourne]
- Pin H1 ("Paint Correction Melbourne") to Position 1 as keyword alignment safeguard
- Leave remaining headlines unpinned — allow machine learning to discover best combinations across H2–H5
- Pin D2 (Melbourne summer urgency CTA) to Description Position 1
- Bidding: Target CPA, with enhanced CPC as fallback if conversion data is thin (less than 30 conversions)

**Campaign-level audience layers (observation, not targeting-only):**
1. Remarketing — Pristine website visitors (all pages, last 30 days)
2. Customer match — confirmed past Pristine customers (observe; exclude from new booking campaigns if already booked for summer)
3. Similar audiences — based on past booking converters

Bid modifier guidance: +25% on [paint correction Toorak] and similar suburb-specific terms. The suburb qualifier is a strong ICP signal — Toorak, South Yarra, Brighton, and Hawthorn car owners searching with postcodes are deeper in the funnel than a generic Melbourne searcher.
